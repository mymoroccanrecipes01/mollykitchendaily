No-Bake Fudgy Brownie Bites with a Hint of Sea Salt

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 5, 2026
15 min Prep Time
12 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These no-bake brownie bites are a quick chocolate fix that’s fudgy and rich without heating up the kitchen. The sprinkle of sea salt on top gives a lovely balance that keeps you coming back for more. Perfect for a last-minute treat or casual get-together!

Introduction

I first stumbled on this no-bake brownie bites recipe when I wanted something chocolaty but didn’t want to heat up the kitchen during a hot summer day. What I love about this version is how simple and quick it is—no oven, no fuss, just blending a few wholesome ingredients. Unlike traditional brownies, these bites rely on dates and walnuts for that fudgy texture, making them naturally sweet and packed with good stuff. The trick I learned is soaking the dates beforehand; it softens them so the mixture blends smoothly without any gritty bits. Plus, the sprinkle of sea salt on top adds this amazing contrast that really wakes up the chocolate flavor. These bites are rich but not overly sweet, so they make a perfect afternoon snack or a casual dessert when friends pop by. Honestly, I’ve made them several times now and each batch disappears fast. They look rustic and homemade, which I think makes them even more inviting. If you’re looking for a quick, healthier chocolate fix that’s easy to whip up and requires zero baking, this recipe is a keeper. I can’t wait for you to try it and see how addictive these brownie bites are!

What You'll Need

Approximately 12 brownie bites

  • 1 cup pitted Medjool dates, soaked in warm water for 10 minutes and drained
  • 1/2 cup raw walnuts
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder, unsweetened
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Sea salt flakes, for topping

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by soaking your dates in warm water for about 10 minutes. This softens them up and makes them easier to blend into a smooth, sticky paste. If you skip this step, your mixture might end up gritty or dry, which ruins the fudgy texture we’re aiming for. After soaking, drain the dates well and pat them dry with a paper towel to avoid excess moisture in the batter.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Dates are plump and soft to the touch, no longer firm or dry.
Step 2
Place the soaked dates, walnuts, cocoa powder, salt, and vanilla extract into a food processor. Pulse until the mixture comes together into a sticky, coarse dough. Don’t over-process or you’ll end up with a paste that’s too smooth and loses texture. The walnuts should still be a bit chunky to give the bites some nice crunch and body.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Mixture sticks together when pressed but still has small walnut pieces visible.
Step 3
Line a baking sheet or plate with parchment paper. Using your hands, scoop out about a tablespoon of the mixture and roll it into a ball. Press lightly to form a bite-sized brownie shape. If the mixture feels too sticky, chill it in the fridge for 10 minutes to firm up, which makes rolling easier.
⏱ Time: 8
✓ Doneness: Brownie bites hold their shape without crumbling or sticking excessively to your hands.
Step 4
Place the brownie bites on the lined tray and sprinkle each with a pinch of sea salt flakes. This final touch enhances the chocolate's depth and balances the sweetness. Don’t skip the sea salt—it’s what makes these bites stand out from typical no-bake treats.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Even, light salt flakes visible on each brownie bite.
Step 5
Refrigerate the brownie bites for at least 30 minutes to firm up. This chilling step helps the bites set and develop that perfect fudgy texture. If you eat them right away, they might feel too soft or sticky.
⏱ Time: 30
✓ Doneness: Bites are firm to the touch but still soft and chewy inside.
Step 6
Once chilled, transfer the bites to an airtight container. Keep them refrigerated until ready to serve to maintain their texture and freshness. They’ll keep well for up to a week, making them a great make-ahead snack.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Stored properly, bites remain moist and delicious without drying out.
Step 7
For serving, let the bites sit at room temperature for about 5 minutes if they feel too firm from the fridge. This softens them slightly, releasing their chocolatey aroma and making them easier to bite into.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Bites are soft and chewy but still hold their shape.
Step 8
If you want to mix things up, try rolling the bites in crushed nuts, shredded coconut, or cocoa powder before chilling. This adds texture and extra flavor layers, making the bites more visually appealing and interesting to eat.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Even coating on the outside with no loose bits falling off.
Step 9
Enjoy your no-bake brownie bites with a cup of coffee or tea for a cozy afternoon treat. They also make great party snacks or lunchbox surprises. Just remember, a little goes a long way—they’re rich and satisfying!
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Ready to eat and delightfully fudgy with a salty finish.

Variations

Coconut-Covered Brownie Bites

Roll the finished brownie bites in unsweetened shredded coconut before chilling. This adds a tropical twist and a pleasant chewy texture on the outside, complementing the fudgy interior. Great if you want a lighter, slightly nutty flavor profile.

Note: Ensure the bites are slightly firm before rolling to avoid coconut sticking unevenly. Chill immediately after coating.

Espresso Brownie Bites

Add 1 teaspoon of instant espresso powder to the batter for a mocha flavor. The coffee enhances the chocolate’s richness and adds depth. Perfect for coffee lovers looking for a sophisticated twist.

Note: Dissolve the espresso powder in the vanilla extract or a teaspoon of warm water before blending to avoid grainy texture.

Peanut Butter Swirl Brownie Bites

Before rolling, fold in 2 tablespoons of creamy peanut butter into the mixture for a nutty, creamy swirl. This variation adds richness and a complementary flavor that pairs beautifully with chocolate.

Note: Gently fold in peanut butter at the end to keep the texture slightly marbled rather than fully mixed.

Spiced Brownie Bites

Add 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon and a pinch of cayenne pepper to the batter for a warm, spicy kick. This adds complexity and a subtle heat that contrasts nicely with the sweet chocolate.

Note: Mix spices thoroughly with the cocoa powder before adding to ensure even distribution.

Vegan Protein Brownie Bites

Add 2 tablespoons of your favorite plant-based protein powder to the mixture for an extra boost. This makes the bites more filling and suitable as a post-workout snack without sacrificing flavor.

Note: Choose an unflavored or chocolate-flavored protein powder and adjust dates slightly if the mixture feels too dry.

Pro Tips

Always soak the dates before blending.
Soaking softens the fibrous texture of the dates, which helps them blend into a smooth paste. Without soaking, the mixture can be gritty and dry, making it harder to form cohesive brownie bites. This step ensures the right sticky consistency that binds the ingredients together naturally.
Don’t over-process the mixture in the food processor.
Over-blending can cause the mixture to become too smooth and paste-like, losing the pleasant texture provided by chopped walnuts. A little chunkiness adds crunch and interest, so pulse just until the ingredients hold together but still have visible walnut pieces.
Use flaky sea salt instead of fine table salt.
Flaky sea salt adds a delicate crunch and bursts of saltiness without overwhelming the bites. Fine salt can dissolve too quickly and make the bites taste overly salty. The flakes also look pretty sprinkled on top, enhancing presentation.
Chill the bites for at least 30 minutes before serving.
Chilling helps the fats in the walnuts and the sticky date mixture firm up, giving the bites a fudgy texture that's easy to handle. Eating them immediately after shaping can feel too soft or sticky, so patience here improves texture and flavor melding.
Store the brownie bites in an airtight container in the fridge.
This keeps them fresh and prevents drying out. The cool temperature also maintains their fudgy texture. Leaving them uncovered or at room temperature for long periods can cause them to become hard or lose moisture.
Experiment with rolling bites in shredded coconut or crushed nuts.
Adding coatings introduces new textures and flavors, making the bites more visually appealing and interesting to eat. This variation is quick to do and adds a nice finishing touch without complicating the recipe.
Use fresh walnuts for the best flavor.
Walnuts can go rancid quickly if stored improperly. Fresh walnuts provide a rich, nutty flavor and healthy oils essential for the fudgy texture. Stale walnuts taste bitter and can ruin the overall flavor of the bites.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the date soaking step

Why it happens Sometimes, cooks assume dates can be blended dry like other nuts or powders, not realizing how fibrous and dense they are.

Fix Always soak dates in warm water for at least 10 minutes before using. This softens their texture, making them blend smoothly and bind the mixture properly. If you’re short on time, soak in hot water to speed the process.

Over-blending the mixture

Why it happens It's tempting to blend everything until completely smooth, especially if you want a uniform texture.

Fix Pulse the food processor in short bursts until the mixture just comes together but still has small walnut chunks. This keeps the bites from becoming pasty and maintains a pleasant texture contrast.

Using too much salt or the wrong kind

Why it happens Some cooks use fine table salt thinking it will dissolve evenly, but it can quickly overpower the delicate sweet and chocolate flavors.

Fix Use flaky sea salt sparingly on top for bursts of flavor. Avoid mixing salt into the batter. This approach balances sweetness and saltiness perfectly.

Not chilling the bites before serving

Why it happens Impatience or lack of planning can lead to skipping the chilling step.

Fix Refrigerate the bites for at least 30 minutes to set their texture. If served immediately, they may be sticky and difficult to handle.

Storing bites improperly

Why it happens Sometimes bites are left uncovered or stored at room temperature for too long.

Fix Store in an airtight container in the fridge to keep them fresh and maintain texture. Avoid leaving them at room temperature for more than a few hours.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Brownie bites can safely stay at room temperature for up to 4 hours if covered to protect from dust and insects. Beyond that, they may dry out or become too soft, so refrigeration is recommended for longer storage.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 7 days. The bites will firm up and maintain their fudgy texture. If served cold, they have a pleasantly chewy texture but can be softened slightly by letting them sit at room temperature for 5 minutes before eating.

Freezer

Freeze the brownie bites in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to a freezer-safe container or bag. Freeze for up to 2 months. To thaw, place in the fridge overnight or at room temperature for 1-2 hours. Avoid microwaving to prevent texture loss.

Reheating

These bites are best enjoyed cold or at room temperature. If you prefer them softer, let them sit out for 5-10 minutes. Avoid microwaving as it can make the texture gummy or overly soft.

Make Ahead

Prepare the mixture and roll the bites 1-2 days ahead, then store them in the fridge covered. Add the sea salt topping just before serving to keep the flakes crisp and visually appealing.

FAQ

Can I use other nuts instead of walnuts?
Absolutely! Pecans or almonds work well and provide a different flavor profile. Just keep in mind that the texture and oil content vary, so you might need to adjust the amount slightly. Pecans offer a buttery taste, while almonds give a firmer crunch.
What if I don’t have a food processor?
You can use a high-powered blender, but you may need to stop and scrape the sides frequently. Alternatively, finely chop the nuts and mash the dates by hand, then mix everything thoroughly. The texture might be a bit chunkier but still delicious.
How do I store these brownie bites if I’m taking them to a party?
Pack them in an airtight container and keep them chilled in a cooler if possible. If not, try to keep them out of direct sunlight and consume within 4 hours to maintain texture and freshness.
Can I add sweeteners like honey or maple syrup?
Since dates are naturally sweet, additional sweeteners usually aren’t necessary. However, if you prefer a sweeter bite, a tablespoon of maple syrup or honey can be added. Just reduce the dates slightly to keep the texture balanced.
Why do the bites sometimes turn out too sticky?
This usually means there’s excess moisture in the mixture, possibly from not draining the soaked dates well or over-soaking. Make sure to drain and pat the dates dry. Chilling the mixture before rolling also helps reduce stickiness.
Can I make these gluten-free?
Yes! This recipe is naturally gluten-free as it contains no flour or gluten ingredients. Just ensure any add-ins or coatings like shredded coconut are certified gluten-free if you have sensitivities.
Is it okay to omit the sea salt topping?
You can omit it if you prefer, but the sea salt adds a wonderful contrast that enhances the chocolate flavor. If you don’t have sea salt flakes, a small pinch of fine salt mixed into the batter can be a substitute.
How long do these brownie bites last in the freezer?
They keep well for up to 2 months in the freezer when properly stored in airtight containers or freezer bags. Beyond that, they may lose flavor and texture quality. Thaw them slowly in the fridge for best results.
