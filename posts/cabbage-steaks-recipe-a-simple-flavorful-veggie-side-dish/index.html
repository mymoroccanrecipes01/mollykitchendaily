Simple Roasted Cabbage Steaks with Garlic and Olive Oil

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 15, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These roasted cabbage steaks are a cozy, hearty side that’s easy to make. Olive oil and garlic give them a lovely flavor, crisping the edges just right. Perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or alongside your favorite protein.

Introduction

I first tried roasted cabbage steaks a few years ago when I was looking for a vegetable side that felt a little more substantial but still simple. Honestly, I wasn’t sure how well cabbage would hold up when roasted in thick slices, but the results pleasantly surprised me. The trick I learned is to slice the cabbage thick enough so it doesn’t fall apart but thin enough to get tender in the oven. Also, tossing the slices with plenty of olive oil and garlic before roasting really brings out the flavor and helps get those golden, crispy edges that make this dish so addictive. This version is different from the usual boiled or sautéed cabbage because it develops a sweet, caramelized flavor and a lovely texture contrast. It’s perfect alongside roasted meats or as a cozy vegetarian dinner with some crusty bread. Every time I make this, my family asks for seconds. Plus, it’s one of those recipes that looks fancy but is super easy to whip up on a busy weeknight.

What You'll Need

Approximately 4 servings

  • 1 large head of green cabbage, cut into 1-inch thick slices
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: 1 teaspoon smoked paprika or chili flakes for a kick

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). This high temperature is key to getting those cabbage edges nicely browned and a bit crispy. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat to prevent sticking. Make sure the oven is fully heated before putting the cabbage in to ensure even roasting.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Oven fully preheated and baking sheet ready to go
Step 2
Slice the cabbage into 1-inch thick rounds, trying to keep them intact as much as possible. Thinner slices might fall apart, and thicker ones may not cook through evenly. Pat the slices dry with a clean towel to remove excess moisture, which helps them roast instead of steam.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cabbage slices are uniform thickness and dry to the touch
Step 3
In a small bowl, mix 2 tablespoons of olive oil with the minced garlic, salt, pepper, and optional smoked paprika or chili flakes if using. This flavorful oil mixture will coat the cabbage and infuse it with garlicky goodness as it roasts.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Oil mixture is well combined and aromatic
Step 4
Brush both sides of each cabbage steak generously with the garlic olive oil mixture. Don’t be shy here—this coating is what helps the cabbage caramelize and develop those crispy edges. Arrange the steaks in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet without overlapping.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Steaks evenly coated and spaced on the baking sheet
Step 5
Roast the cabbage steaks in the preheated oven for 20 minutes. Halfway through, carefully flip each steak over using tongs or a spatula to ensure even browning on both sides. Keep an eye on them to avoid burning—roasting develops a sweet, nutty flavor when done right.
⏱ Time: 20 minutes
✓ Doneness: Edges are browned and steaks feel tender when pierced with a fork
Step 6
While the cabbage roasts, toss the remaining tablespoon of olive oil with a pinch of salt and pepper to drizzle over the finished steaks for extra richness and shine. This last step adds a fresh hit of flavor and prevents them from drying out once out of the oven.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Oil is ready to drizzle and seasoned
Step 7
Once roasted, remove the cabbage steaks from the oven and transfer to a serving platter. Drizzle with the reserved olive oil and sprinkle a little extra freshly ground black pepper or flaky sea salt if desired. Serve immediately to enjoy the contrast of crispy edges and tender centers.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Steaks steaming hot with a golden crust
Step 8
Optional: Garnish with fresh herbs like parsley or thyme for a bright touch. These herbs add freshness that balances the richness of the olive oil and garlic. Plus, they make the dish look even more inviting on the plate.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Fresh herbs scattered evenly over the steaks
Step 9
Serve your roasted cabbage steaks warm as a side to grilled meats, roasted chicken, or even your favorite grain bowl. They’re best eaten the day they’re made but can be reheated gently if needed. Enjoy the cozy, garlicky flavors with every bite!
⏱ Time: Immediate
✓ Doneness: Steaks plated and ready to enjoy

Variations

Spicy Roasted Cabbage Steaks

Add a pinch of cayenne pepper or chili flakes to the olive oil and garlic mixture before roasting. This gives the cabbage a nice heat kick that pairs well with smoky paprika. Expect a bolder, spicier flavor profile that livens up the dish.

Note: Be cautious with spice amounts to avoid overpowering the natural sweetness of the cabbage. Start small and adjust to taste.

Cheesy Roasted Cabbage Steaks

Sprinkle grated Parmesan or sharp cheddar over the cabbage steaks during the last 5 minutes of roasting. The cheese melts and crisps, adding a savory, umami richness that’s irresistible.

Note: Watch closely to prevent cheese from burning; add cheese midway through the final roasting phase.

Lemon Herb Roasted Cabbage

Finish the roasted steaks with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and a sprinkle of chopped fresh herbs like dill or parsley. This brightens the dish and adds a refreshing contrast to the roasted flavors.

Note: Add lemon juice just before serving to preserve its fresh, vibrant taste.

Balsamic Glazed Cabbage Steaks

Drizzle a little balsamic vinegar or glaze over the cabbage right after roasting for a sweet-tart flavor twist. This adds depth and pairs beautifully with the caramelized edges.

Note: Use glaze sparingly to avoid sogginess; apply just before serving.

Vegan Roasted Cabbage with Tahini Drizzle

Top the roasted cabbage steaks with a simple tahini sauce mixed with lemon and garlic. This adds a creamy, nutty element and makes the dish feel more substantial as a vegan main or side.

Note: Prepare the tahini sauce ahead and drizzle just before serving to maintain texture.

Pro Tips

Make sure to dry the cabbage slices thoroughly before roasting.
Excess moisture on the cabbage causes steaming instead of roasting, which prevents the edges from crisping up and dilutes the flavor. Patting the slices dry ensures the heat can brown the surface properly, resulting in those desirable caramelized edges and a better texture contrast.
Flip the cabbage steaks halfway through roasting for even browning.
Roasting one side only leads to uneven cooking and can cause one side to burn while the other remains pale and undercooked. Turning the steaks ensures both sides get equal exposure to the hot oven air, producing a balanced texture and flavor throughout.
Use a sharp knife to slice the cabbage cleanly and evenly.
A clean cut prevents the cabbage leaves from shredding or falling apart. Uneven slices cook unevenly, with thinner parts drying out and thicker parts staying tough. A sharp knife helps maintain the integrity of the steaks, which is key for roasting them successfully.
Don’t overcrowd the baking sheet; leave space between cabbage steaks.
Crowding traps steam and reduces airflow, causing the cabbage to steam rather than roast. Proper spacing allows hot air to circulate freely around each steak, promoting even caramelization and crisping.
Use parchment paper or a silicone mat to line your baking sheet.
This prevents sticking and makes cleanup much easier. Without a liner, the cabbage’s natural sugars can stick to the pan and burn, creating a bitter taste and making it difficult to remove the steaks intact.
Add fresh herbs like thyme or parsley right after roasting.
Herbs add a fresh, bright note that balances the rich, roasted flavors. Adding them after cooking preserves their delicate aroma and color, enhancing both the taste and visual appeal of the dish.
Use good quality olive oil for the best flavor and roasting results.
High-quality olive oil has a higher smoke point and richer flavor, which helps develop the cabbage’s caramelization without burning. Cheap oils may impart off-flavors or fail to crisp the edges properly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using thin cabbage slices that fall apart during roasting.

Why it happens Many cooks think thinner slices will cook faster, but cabbage’s layered structure means thin slices lose integrity and become mushy when roasted.

Fix Slice the cabbage into about 1-inch thick steaks to maintain shape and texture. If you slice thinner, expect a softer, less appealing texture and adjust cooking time accordingly.

Not preheating the oven fully before roasting.

Why it happens Rushing the process or underestimating oven heat leads to uneven cooking and lack of crispiness.

Fix Always preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C) before placing the cabbage inside. This ensures immediate high heat contact, promoting caramelization.

Skipping the flipping step halfway through roasting.

Why it happens Forgetting to turn the steaks often happens when multitasking or assuming one side will brown enough.

Fix Set a timer to remind yourself to flip the cabbage steaks after 10 minutes. This guarantees even browning and prevents burning on one side.

Using too little oil, resulting in dry, bland cabbage.

Why it happens Trying to cut calories or assuming less oil means healthier food can make the cabbage dry and unappealing.

Fix Be generous with olive oil—it’s essential for flavor development and texture. The oil helps crisp edges and infuses garlic flavor.

Overcrowding the baking sheet causing steaming rather than roasting.

Why it happens Trying to cook everything at once can trap moisture and prevent browning.

Fix Use two baking sheets if necessary, leaving space between cabbage steaks to allow air circulation and proper roasting.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Roasted cabbage steaks can be left at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered loosely with foil or a clean kitchen towel. Beyond this, bacteria growth increases and it’s safer to refrigerate.

Refrigerator

Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The cabbage will lose some crispness but reheating gently in the oven or stovetop helps restore texture. Avoid microwaving if possible, as it can make the cabbage soggy.

Freezer

You can freeze roasted cabbage steaks for up to 1 month. Wrap each steak individually in plastic wrap then place in a freezer-safe bag to prevent freezer burn. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.

Reheating

Reheat in a 350°F oven for 10-15 minutes to regain some crispness. You can also warm them in a skillet over medium heat with a splash of olive oil. Avoid microwaving directly as it tends to make the cabbage mushy.

Make Ahead

Prepare the cabbage steaks and garlic oil mixture up to 1 day ahead and store separately in the fridge. Assemble and roast fresh on the day you plan to serve for best texture and flavor.

FAQ

Can I use red cabbage instead of green cabbage?
Yes, red cabbage works well for roasting and adds a beautiful color to your dish. It has a slightly peppery flavor and similar texture, so just slice it the same thickness and follow the recipe as is. The cooking time remains the same.
How do I prevent the cabbage from becoming mushy?
Dry the cabbage slices thoroughly before roasting and avoid overcrowding the pan. High heat roasting with enough oil helps create a crispy exterior, preventing mushiness. Also, flipping halfway ensures even cooking and texture.
Can I prepare the cabbage steaks ahead of time?
You can slice the cabbage and prepare the garlic oil mixture a day ahead, but wait to combine and roast until just before serving. This keeps the cabbage fresh and prevents it from becoming soggy or losing flavor.
What if I don’t have smoked paprika?
Smoked paprika adds a subtle smoky depth but is optional. You can substitute with regular paprika or a pinch of cumin for a different flavor profile, or simply omit it and rely on the garlic and olive oil.
Is this recipe vegan and gluten-free?
Yes! This recipe uses just vegetables, olive oil, garlic, and seasonings, making it naturally vegan and gluten-free. It’s a great option for those with dietary restrictions or anyone wanting a healthy side.
How do I get the edges extra crispy?
Make sure the oven is fully preheated to a high temperature and don’t overcrowd the pan. Using enough olive oil and flipping the steaks halfway through roasting promotes even caramelization and crispiness.
Can I add other seasonings or spices?
Absolutely! Feel free to experiment with herbs like thyme, rosemary, or spices such as cumin, chili flakes, or curry powder. Just mix them into the olive oil and garlic before coating the cabbage.
What’s the best way to serve these cabbage steaks?
They’re perfect alongside roasted or grilled meats, fish, or as part of a vegetarian meal with grains like quinoa or rice. Adding a fresh herb garnish or a squeeze of lemon brightens the dish beautifully.
