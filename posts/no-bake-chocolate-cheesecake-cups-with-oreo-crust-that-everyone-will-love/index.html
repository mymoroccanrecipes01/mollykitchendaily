No Bake Chocolate Cheesecake Cups with Crunchy Oreo Crust

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 5, 2026
15 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These no bake chocolate cheesecake cups are creamy, rich, and super easy to whip up. The Oreo crust adds a perfect crunch, making it a fuss-free dessert that feels fancy. Great for family nights or casual get-togethers!

Introduction

I first made these no bake chocolate cheesecake cups on a whim when I wanted something sweet but didn’t want to heat up the kitchen. My family loved them so much that they've become a go-to dessert for busy weeknights and casual gatherings. What makes this version special is the crunchy Oreo crust that adds texture, perfectly balancing the silky chocolate cheesecake filling. I like to melt the chocolate separately and fold it gently into the cream cheese mixture to keep it light and smooth. The cold heavy cream whipped and folded in creates that luscious, airy texture I adore. These cups come together quickly, no oven needed, and look so pretty served in clear glasses. Plus, they store beautifully if you want to prep ahead. The first time I made them, I accidentally overmixed the filling and it got a bit dense — so I learned that gentle folding is key. The end result is rich but not too heavy, with a perfect balance of sweet and chocolatey flavors. Honestly, they’re a crowd-pleaser every time!

What You'll Need

6 individual cheesecake cups

  • 24 Oreo cookies, finely crushed
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 16 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup heavy cream, cold
  • 4 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, melted and cooled

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by crushing the Oreo cookies into fine crumbs using a food processor or by placing them in a sealed bag and smashing with a rolling pin. Then, mix the crumbs thoroughly with the melted butter until the mixture holds together like wet sand. This step is crucial because the butter acts as a binder, helping the crust set firmly without baking. Press the crumb mixture evenly into the bottom of your serving cups or glasses, creating a compact base. Avoid pressing too hard or unevenly, which can cause the crust to crumble when serving.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: The crust should be evenly packed and slightly firm to the touch.
Step 2
In a large mixing bowl, beat the softened cream cheese until smooth and creamy. Gradually add the powdered sugar and vanilla extract, continuing to mix until fully combined. This gradual mixing ensures the sugar dissolves well and the texture stays silky without lumps. If you rush this step, the filling can be grainy or uneven. Stop once the mixture is smooth and fluffy.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: The mixture should be creamy, smooth, and free of lumps.
Step 3
Melt the semi-sweet chocolate gently, either in a microwave in short bursts or over a double boiler. Let it cool slightly before folding it into the cream cheese mixture. This prevents the chocolate from seizing or melting the cream cheese too much. Folding gently preserves the airy texture you want in the cheesecake filling. Avoid stirring vigorously, which can deflate the mixture.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Chocolate should be fully incorporated with no streaks visible.
Step 4
Whip the cold heavy cream in a separate chilled bowl until soft peaks form. This means the cream should hold its shape but still be slightly soft when you lift the whisk. Overwhipping can turn it grainy or into butter, so watch closely. The cold cream helps create a light, fluffy texture in the filling when folded in.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Soft peaks that gently hold their shape without stiffness.
Step 5
Carefully fold the whipped cream into the chocolate cream cheese mixture in batches. Use a spatula and a gentle folding motion to combine without deflating the mixture. This step is key to keeping your cheesecake filling light and airy. If you mix too aggressively, you’ll lose volume and end up with a dense texture.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Filling is smooth, homogeneous, and fluffy without streaks.
Step 6
Spoon or pipe the cheesecake filling over the Oreo crust in your cups, filling them nearly to the top. Level the surface gently with a spatula for a neat finish. This presentation makes the dessert look polished and inviting. Don’t overfill, as the filling will set slightly when chilled.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Each cup is filled evenly with a smooth, level surface.
Step 7
Refrigerate the assembled cheesecake cups for at least 4 hours or overnight. This chilling time allows the filling to set properly and the crust to firm up. Rushing this step will result in a runny filling and crumbly crust. Patience here really pays off for texture and flavor.
⏱ Time: 240
✓ Doneness: Filling is firm to the touch and crust holds its shape when served.
Step 8
Before serving, you can add optional toppings like shaved chocolate, whipped cream, or fresh berries to elevate the dessert. This adds a fresh contrast and makes it look stunning. If adding berries, do so just before serving to avoid sogginess.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Toppings look fresh and vibrant without moisture seeping into filling.
Step 9
Serve chilled straight from the fridge. Use a spoon to dig through the crunchy Oreo crust and creamy chocolate filling. The contrast in textures and the rich taste make this dessert a winner every time. Leftovers can be stored covered in the fridge for a couple of days.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Dessert is cold, firm, and holds its layers when spooned.

Variations

Peanut Butter Chocolate Cheesecake Cups

Swap half the cream cheese with creamy peanut butter for a delicious twist. The peanut butter adds richness and a nutty flavor that pairs perfectly with chocolate and Oreo crust. Expect a thicker, more decadent filling.

Note: Peanut butter thickens the filling, so fold gently and consider chilling longer for best texture.

Mint Chocolate Cheesecake Cups

Add 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract to the filling for a refreshing minty kick. Garnish with crushed peppermint candies or fresh mint leaves to enhance the flavor and presentation.

Note: Peppermint extract is strong—start with a small amount and adjust to taste to avoid overpowering the chocolate.

Berry Topped No Bake Cheesecake Cups

Top each cup with fresh mixed berries like raspberries, blueberries, or strawberries before serving. The tart fruit balances the rich chocolate and adds a pop of color and freshness.

Note: Add berries just before serving to prevent sogginess and maintain texture contrast.

Gluten-Free Chocolate Cheesecake Cups

Use gluten-free chocolate sandwich cookies for the crust to make this dessert suitable for gluten-sensitive eaters. The texture and flavor remain just as satisfying and delicious.

Note: Ensure all other ingredients are gluten-free certified to avoid cross-contamination.

Vegan No Bake Chocolate Cheesecake

Replace cream cheese with vegan cream cheese and use coconut cream instead of heavy cream. Use dairy-free chocolate to keep it fully vegan. The texture will be slightly different but still creamy and rich.

Note: Chill longer to help the vegan filling set properly as it tends to be softer.

Pro Tips

Use room temperature cream cheese for a smooth filling.
Room temperature cream cheese blends more easily with sugar and other ingredients, resulting in a lump-free, silky filling. If it’s too cold, you’ll get clumps that are hard to smooth out, which affects the texture.
Don’t overwhip the heavy cream; stop at soft peaks.
Soft peaks mean the cream is light and airy but still flexible enough to fold into the filling without deflating it. Overwhipped cream turns grainy and stiff, which can ruin the mousse-like texture of the cheesecake.
Press the Oreo crust firmly into the cups.
Firm pressure helps the butter and crumbs bind tightly, so the crust holds together when you eat the cheesecake. Loose crust will crumble and fall apart, making the dessert messy to serve.
Chill the cheesecake cups overnight if possible.
Chilling sets the filling and crust properly, improving texture and flavor melding. If rushed, the filling may be too soft and the crust crumbly.
Melt chocolate gently to avoid burning.
Chocolate burns easily when heated too fast, which creates a bitter taste and grainy texture. Using a double boiler or microwave in short bursts helps melt it smoothly.
Fold ingredients gently to keep filling airy.
Gentle folding preserves the air whipped into the cream and cream cheese mixture, ensuring a light, fluffy texture rather than a dense, heavy one.
Use clear serving glasses to show layers.
Visual appeal makes dessert more inviting. The contrast between the dark Oreo crust and creamy chocolate filling looks stunning in transparent cups.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Overmixing the filling

Why it happens Many people mix vigorously trying to combine ingredients quickly, but this deflates the whipped cream and makes the filling dense.

Fix Use a spatula to fold ingredients gently and stop mixing as soon as everything is combined. This keeps the filling light and airy.

Using cold cream cheese

Why it happens Rushing to make the recipe without softening cream cheese leads to lumps and uneven texture.

Fix Plan ahead and leave cream cheese out for at least 30 minutes before starting, or soften slightly in the microwave in short bursts.

Pressing the crust too loosely

Why it happens Not pressing the Oreo crust firmly means it won’t hold shape and will crumble when serving.

Fix Use the back of a spoon or a small glass to firmly press the crust into place until compact.

Skipping the chilling step

Why it happens Impatience leads to serving before the filling and crust set properly, resulting in a runny dessert.

Fix Chill for at least 4 hours or overnight to allow the texture to firm up and flavors to meld.

Adding hot melted chocolate directly

Why it happens Hot chocolate can melt the cream cheese and cream, ruining texture.

Fix Let melted chocolate cool to room temperature before folding it into the filling.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These no bake cheesecake cups can be kept at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered to prevent drying out. Beyond that, they should be refrigerated to maintain texture and safety.

Refrigerator

Store the cheesecake cups in an airtight container or cover with plastic wrap in the fridge for up to 3 days. The crust may soften slightly over time, but the flavor remains great. Serve chilled or let sit for 10 minutes at room temp before eating.

Freezer

Freeze cheesecake cups in airtight containers for up to 1 month. Wrap cups individually with plastic wrap and place in a freezer-safe container to prevent freezer burn. Thaw in the fridge overnight before serving. Texture may be slightly less creamy after freezing.

Reheating

No reheating needed—these are best served chilled. If desired, let sit at room temperature for 10-15 minutes to soften slightly before serving.

Make Ahead

Prepare crust and filling up to 1 day ahead. Assemble cups and refrigerate overnight to save time. Add fresh toppings just before serving for best presentation.

FAQ

Can I use another kind of cookie for the crust?
Absolutely! While Oreos give a classic chocolate flavor and crunchy texture, you can substitute with graham crackers, chocolate wafers, or any cookie you like. Just crush them finely and mix with melted butter to create a firm crust. Keep in mind that flavor and sweetness will vary depending on the cookie choice.
What if I don’t have heavy cream? Can I use something else?
Heavy cream is important because it whips into soft peaks, adding lightness to the filling. If you don’t have it, you could try whipping chilled coconut cream as a dairy-free alternative. Milk or half-and-half won’t whip properly and will change the texture, making it denser.
How long can I store these cheesecake cups?
They keep well in the refrigerator for up to 3 days if covered properly. After that, the crust may get soggy and the filling can lose its fresh texture. For longer storage, freezing is an option, but thaw completely before serving for best results.
Can I make these gluten-free?
Yes, simply swap the Oreo cookies for gluten-free sandwich cookies or another gluten-free cookie option. Make sure the rest of your ingredients are gluten-free certified to avoid cross-contamination.
Why is my filling grainy?
Graininess usually happens if the cream cheese isn’t fully softened or if the sugar isn’t well incorporated. Make sure the cream cheese is at room temperature and beat it well before adding other ingredients. Also, sift powdered sugar if you notice lumps.
Can I prepare these in larger pans instead of cups?
Definitely! You can assemble this cheesecake in a springform pan or any dish you like. Just press the crust evenly and pour the filling on top. Chill as directed and slice when ready. The texture and flavors remain the same.
Is it necessary to melt the chocolate?
Melting the chocolate before folding it in ensures it blends smoothly without lumps or streaks. Adding unmelted chocolate chips won’t incorporate properly and can affect texture and appearance.
Can I add other flavors to the filling?
Yes! Vanilla extract is classic, but you can try peppermint, orange zest, coffee, or almond extract to customize the flavor. Add small amounts and taste as you go to avoid overpowering the chocolate.
