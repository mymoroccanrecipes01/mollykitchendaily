Baked Jalapeño Poppers with Creamy Cheddar Filling That Everyone Will Love

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 4, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These poppers are stuffed with a dreamy mix of cream cheese and sharp cheddar, baked until golden and bubbly. Perfect for spicing up your snack game during weekend hangouts or game days!

Introduction

I first tried jalapeño poppers at a friend's barbecue years ago, and I was instantly hooked on that creamy, spicy combo. Since then, I've tweaked the recipe a bit to get the perfect balance between heat and cheesy goodness. What I love about this version is the creamy filling made from cream cheese and sharp cheddar – it melts so nicely and keeps the poppers moist. A little garlic powder and smoked paprika add a subtle depth that makes these poppers stand out. Plus, topping them with crunchy panko breadcrumbs before baking gives a wonderful golden crust that's just irresistible. I’ve had my share of overcooked poppers that turned dry or under-seasoned ones that felt flat, so I always make sure to watch the baking time closely and season the filling well. When you bite into these, you’ll get that satisfying contrast of crisp skin, creamy cheese, and a spicy kick. They’re perfect for game days, casual parties, or just when you want a cozy snack that feels a little special. Trust me, once you make these, they’ll be a staple in your snack rotation!

What You'll Need

Approximately 24 poppers, serves 6

  • 12 fresh jalapeño peppers, halved lengthwise and seeded
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by preheating your oven to 400°F (200°C). While it warms up, slice the jalapeños in half lengthwise and carefully remove the seeds and membranes. This step is crucial because it controls the heat level—if you want it milder, remove all seeds; for more spice, leave a few in. Use a spoon or a small knife, and be careful not to tear the peppers, as they need to hold the filling well.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Jalapeño halves should be cleanly halved with seeds removed but intact flesh
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, combine the softened cream cheese and shredded cheddar. Add garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly until smooth and creamy. This blend is the heart of your poppers, so make sure the cream cheese is soft enough to mix easily. Seasoning here is key to balance the heat of the jalapeños, so taste a small bit of the filling and adjust if needed.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Filling should be smooth and evenly mixed with no lumps
Step 3
Spoon the cheese mixture generously into each jalapeño half, pressing gently but not overstuffing. Overfilling can cause the filling to spill out during baking, while underfilling results in dry bites. Make sure each popper is filled to the edges for maximum cheesy goodness.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Jalapeños filled evenly, with cheese level just below the edges
Step 4
Place the filled jalapeños on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper or a silicone mat to prevent sticking. This also makes cleanup easier. Arrange them so they’re not touching to allow even heat circulation around each popper.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Poppers spaced evenly on the baking sheet
Step 5
In a small bowl, toss the panko breadcrumbs with olive oil until evenly coated. This step ensures the breadcrumbs brown nicely and add a crisp topping. Sprinkle the breadcrumb mixture evenly over each filled jalapeño. The oil helps create that golden crunch without drying out the filling.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Breadcrumbs look evenly coated with oil and spread uniformly on poppers
Step 6
Bake the poppers in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes. Watch closely in the last 5 minutes; you want the cheese to be bubbly and the breadcrumbs golden brown. Baking too long can dry the peppers, so start checking early if your oven runs hot.
⏱ Time: 20-25 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cheese filling is bubbly and breadcrumbs are golden and crisp
Step 7
Remove the poppers from the oven and let them cool for about 5 minutes before serving. This resting time allows the filling to set slightly, making them easier to handle and enhancing the flavor.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Poppers are warm but not piping hot, filling slightly firmed up
Step 8
Serve the jalapeño poppers on a platter with a side of sour cream or ranch dressing if you like. The creamy dip pairs wonderfully with the spicy, cheesy poppers and balances the heat perfectly. This step is optional but highly recommended for extra flavor.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Poppers plated attractively with optional dip on the side
Step 9
Enjoy your homemade jalapeño poppers fresh and warm. They make fantastic appetizers or snacks for game days, parties, or whenever you want a little spicy comfort food. Leftovers can be refrigerated but are best eaten within a day or two for optimal flavor and texture.
⏱ Time: Immediate
✓ Doneness: Poppers eaten warm with crispy topping and creamy filling intact

Variations

Bacon-Wrapped Jalapeño Poppers

Wrap each stuffed jalapeño half with a slice of bacon before baking for an extra smoky, savory flavor. The bacon crisps up beautifully and adds a meaty contrast to the creamy filling. Expect a richer, heartier snack that’s perfect for meat lovers.

Note: Increase baking time by 5-7 minutes to ensure bacon is fully cooked and crispy without drying out the filling.

Vegan Jalapeño Poppers

Swap the cream cheese and cheddar for a blend of vegan cream cheese and shredded vegan cheese. Use olive oil or vegan butter in the breadcrumb topping. This variation keeps the spicy, creamy texture but is entirely plant-based.

Note: Vegan cheeses may melt differently; watch baking closely to avoid overcooking or drying out the peppers.

Jalapeño Poppers with Chorizo Filling

Add cooked, crumbled chorizo sausage to the cream cheese and cheddar mixture for a spicy, meaty twist. This adds depth and a smoky punch, making the poppers more filling and flavorful.

Note: Cook chorizo fully before mixing with cheeses. Reduce added salt as chorizo is already salty.

Herbed Jalapeño Poppers

Mix fresh chopped herbs like cilantro, parsley, or chives into the cheese filling for a fresh, bright flavor. This adds a vibrant twist and balances the heat with herbal notes.

Note: Add herbs just before filling to keep them fresh and vibrant; avoid overmixing which can bruise herbs.

Spicy Jalapeño Poppers with Hot Sauce

Stir a few dashes of your favorite hot sauce into the cheese filling to amp up the heat and add tangy complexity. Great if you like an extra kick beyond the jalapeño’s natural spice.

Note: Add hot sauce gradually and taste test to avoid overpowering the creamy texture.

Pro Tips

Wear gloves when handling jalapeños to avoid skin irritation.
Jalapeño oils can cause burning sensations on your skin or eyes. Wearing gloves prevents this and makes handling the peppers safer and more comfortable. If you forget, washing hands thoroughly with soap and avoiding touching your face helps reduce irritation.
Soften the cream cheese well before mixing.
Softened cream cheese blends smoothly with cheddar and seasonings, preventing lumps in the filling. If it’s too cold, the filling will be uneven and harder to spoon into the peppers, affecting texture and appearance.
Don’t overcrowd the baking sheet.
Spacing the poppers evenly allows hot air to circulate freely, ensuring even cooking and browning. Crowding traps moisture and leads to soggy bottoms or unevenly cooked peppers.
Use fresh shredded cheddar instead of pre-shredded.
Pre-shredded cheese often contains anti-caking agents which can affect melting quality and texture. Freshly shredded cheddar melts more smoothly and creates a creamier filling.
Adjust the amount of seeds for heat preference.
Jalapeño seeds and membranes contain most of the heat. Removing all seeds results in milder poppers, while leaving some increases spiciness. This simple adjustment lets you tailor the recipe to your taste.
Let the poppers rest after baking.
Resting allows the cheese filling to firm up slightly, making them easier to handle and eat without the filling spilling out. It also lets flavors meld together for a better taste experience.
Use panko breadcrumbs for the topping.
Panko’s light, flaky texture crisps up beautifully when tossed with oil and baked. Regular breadcrumbs tend to absorb moisture and become soggy, losing the delightful crunch that makes these poppers special.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Not removing enough seeds and membranes

Why it happens Many cooks underestimate how spicy the seeds and membranes are, leading to overly hot poppers that might overwhelm some guests.

Fix Use a small spoon or knife to carefully scrape out all seeds and membranes. Taste a small piece of the pepper beforehand to gauge heat and adjust accordingly. This control ensures your poppers have just the right kick.

Overbaking the poppers

Why it happens It’s easy to leave the poppers in too long trying to get a crispy crust, but this dries out the peppers and filling.

Fix Keep a close eye during the last 5 minutes of baking. Remove them as soon as the cheese is bubbly and the breadcrumbs are golden. This keeps the peppers juicy and the filling creamy.

Using cold cream cheese

Why it happens Cold cream cheese is difficult to mix and creates lumpy filling that doesn’t spread well.

Fix Leave the cream cheese out at room temperature for at least 30 minutes before mixing. This ensures a smooth, even filling that bakes perfectly.

Skipping the olive oil on breadcrumbs

Why it happens Some skip tossing the breadcrumbs in oil, leading to dry, pale, or burnt topping.

Fix Always toss panko with olive oil before sprinkling on the poppers. The oil promotes even browning and a crisp, golden crust.

Overfilling the jalapeños

Why it happens Trying to stuff too much filling causes it to spill out during baking, making a mess and uneven cooking.

Fix Fill the peppers just to the edge without overpacking. This keeps the filling contained and ensures even cooking.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Jalapeño poppers can be left at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered loosely with foil or a clean towel to prevent drying out. Beyond that, bacteria growth increases and they should be refrigerated.

Refrigerator

Store leftover poppers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. They may lose some crispness but reheat well. For best flavor, reheat in the oven to restore crunch rather than microwaving.

Freezer

Freeze poppers in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to a freezer-safe container or bag for up to 2 months. Wrap tightly to prevent freezer burn. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.

Reheating

Reheat poppers in a preheated oven at 375°F (190°C) for 10-15 minutes until warmed through and crispy. Avoid microwaving as it makes the breadcrumbs soggy. Add a splash of water to the baking dish if filling seems dry.

Make Ahead

Prepare and fill the jalapeños up to 24 hours ahead, keep them covered in the fridge. Add breadcrumbs and bake just before serving for freshest texture and flavor.

FAQ

Can I make jalapeño poppers less spicy?
Absolutely! Removing all the seeds and membranes from the jalapeños drastically reduces the heat. You can also choose smaller or milder peppers like mini sweet peppers or poblanos if you want a gentler spice level.
Can I freeze jalapeño poppers before baking?
Yes, you can freeze the stuffed jalapeños before baking. Place them on a baking sheet in a single layer, freeze until solid, then transfer to a freezer-safe container. Bake from frozen, adding a few extra minutes to the cooking time.
What can I substitute for cream cheese in this recipe?
You can use ricotta or mascarpone cheese for a slightly different texture, though cream cheese gives the best creamy melt. For dairy-free options, vegan cream cheese works well but may alter flavor slightly.
How do I prevent the filling from leaking out during baking?
Don’t overfill the jalapeños and make sure the halves are intact without tears. Also, spreading the filling evenly and packing gently helps keep it contained. Baking at the right temperature ensures the filling sets properly.
Can I add other cheeses to the filling?
Definitely! Monterey Jack, mozzarella, or pepper jack can be mixed with cheddar and cream cheese for different flavor profiles. Just make sure to shred fresh cheese for best melting.
Is it necessary to use panko breadcrumbs?
Panko breadcrumbs give the best crispy topping due to their light texture. Regular breadcrumbs can be used but won’t be as crunchy and may become soggy faster.
How long do jalapeño poppers keep in the fridge?
Stored in an airtight container, they keep well for up to 3 days. Reheat in the oven for best texture. Avoid leaving them out longer than 2 hours to maintain food safety.
Can I bake jalapeño poppers on a grill?
Yes! Place the poppers on a grill-safe pan and cook over indirect medium heat with the lid closed. This adds a nice smoky flavor but watch carefully to prevent burning.
