Creamy Avocado Toast Bites with Cherry Tomatoes and Microgreens

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 11, 2026
15 min Prep Time
5 min Time
8 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These avocado toast bites are my go-to for casual get-togethers. Creamy avocado pairs perfectly with juicy cherry tomatoes and fresh microgreens on crisp toasted rounds. Super easy to whip up and share!

Introduction

I first made these avocado toast bites for a last-minute get-together with friends, and they were a hit! What I love about this recipe is how simple yet flavorful it is. The creamy avocado mashed with a bit of lemon juice gives such a fresh base, and topping it with juicy cherry tomatoes and peppery microgreens adds a bright pop of flavor and color. Unlike traditional avocado toast, these bite-sized versions are perfect for sharing and snacking without the mess. I learned that using small toasted rounds keeps everything crisp and easy to eat, which is key when you want to serve a crowd. Plus, the olive oil drizzle ties all the flavors together beautifully. These bites come together in no time, making them my go-to for impromptu parties or casual afternoon snacks. Expect a creamy, fresh, and slightly tangy flavor combo with a satisfying crunch from the toast. They look so pretty on a platter, too—trust me, everyone will be asking for the recipe!

What You'll Need

Approximately 16 bites, serves 8

  • 1 ripe avocado, peeled and pitted
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 16 small toasted bread rounds or crostini
  • 1/4 cup fresh microgreens

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by mashing the ripe avocado in a bowl using a fork until it’s mostly smooth but still has some texture. Add the fresh lemon juice and olive oil, then season with salt and pepper. The lemon juice keeps the avocado from browning and adds a bright tang that balances the richness. Be gentle with the mashing to keep it creamy but not too pasty — you want a spread that holds on the toast but still feels silky.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: The avocado mixture should be creamy with small chunks, bright green, and lightly glossy from the olive oil.
Step 2
Halve the cherry tomatoes and gently toss them with a pinch of salt to bring out their natural sweetness and soften them slightly. This quick step enhances their flavor and prevents the bites from tasting bland. If you skip this, the tomatoes might taste a bit dull or watery, so take the minute to toss them well.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Tomatoes should glisten slightly and smell fresh but not mushy.
Step 3
Toast your bread rounds or crostini until golden and crisp. This is crucial because the crunch contrasts nicely with the creamy avocado and juicy tomatoes. If the toast isn’t crisp enough, the bites can get soggy quickly. Use medium heat so the bread toasts evenly without burning.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Toast should be golden brown and firm to the touch but not blackened.
Step 4
Spread a generous tablespoon of the avocado mixture onto each toasted bread round. Make sure to cover the surface evenly to provide a creamy base for the toppings. An even spread also helps prevent the toast from drying out too fast once topped.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Each toast should have a smooth, even layer of avocado, not too thin and not overly thick.
Step 5
Top each avocado-covered toast with a few halved cherry tomatoes. Arrange them so they don’t fall off easily but still look abundant and inviting. This adds juiciness and a sweet, tangy burst with every bite.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Tomatoes should be evenly distributed and hold their shape on the toast.
Step 6
Add a small handful of fresh microgreens on top of the tomatoes for a peppery, fresh finish. The microgreens give a lovely texture contrast and make these bites look extra special. Be gentle when adding to avoid bruising the greens.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Microgreens should look fresh, vibrant, and not wilted.
Step 7
Drizzle a tiny bit more olive oil over the assembled bites for shine and a subtle richness that enhances all the flavors. This final touch brings everything together beautifully and keeps the bites from feeling dry.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Bites should have a light glossy finish without pooling oil.
Step 8
Season the bites lightly with a pinch of flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper right before serving. This sharpens the flavors and adds a slight crunch from the salt. Don’t salt too early or the avocado might release water and get soggy.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Seasoning should be balanced, enhancing but not overpowering.
Step 9
Serve immediately to enjoy the contrast of textures and freshness. If you need to prep ahead, keep the avocado mixture and bread separate and assemble just before guests arrive to prevent sogginess.
⏱ Time: Immediate
✓ Doneness: Bites should be fresh, crisp, and vibrant looking when served.

Variations

Spicy Avocado Toast Bites

Add a pinch of red pepper flakes or a drizzle of sriracha to the avocado mash for a spicy kick. You can also top with pickled jalapeños for extra heat. This variation adds a lively contrast to the creamy base and makes the bites more exciting.

Note: Be careful not to add too much spice initially; start mild and adjust. The heat should complement, not overpower, the fresh ingredients.

Mediterranean Style Toast Bites

Incorporate crumbled feta cheese and chopped kalamata olives with the avocado. Top with a sprinkle of oregano and a drizzle of balsamic glaze. This version brings savory, salty flavors that pair beautifully with the creamy avocado.

Note: Use firm feta to avoid soggy bites and drizzle balsamic just before serving to keep toast crisp.

Smoked Salmon Avocado Bites

Add a small piece of smoked salmon on top of the avocado spread, then garnish with dill and capers for an elegant twist. This variation makes the bites feel more luxurious and perfect for brunch or special occasions.

Note: Assemble just before serving to preserve salmon’s texture and toast crunch.

Vegan Pesto Avocado Toast Bites

Mix fresh basil pesto into the mashed avocado for an herby, vibrant flavor. Top with sun-dried tomatoes and pine nuts for extra texture and taste. This variation adds a rich, herbal punch while staying vegan-friendly.

Note: Make sure pesto is well blended into avocado for even flavor distribution.

Hearty Grain Toast Bites

Swap white bread rounds for toasted multigrain or rye rounds for a nuttier, earthier base. This adds complexity to the flavor and a satisfying chew, making the bites more filling.

Note: Adjust toasting time as denser breads may take longer to crisp without burning.

Pro Tips

Use ripe but firm avocados for best texture.
Avocados that are too soft become mushy and watery when mashed, leading to a soggy toast base. Firmer, perfectly ripe avocados maintain creaminess while providing enough body to hold on the toast without dripping.
Toast bread rounds just before assembling.
Toasting right before assembly ensures maximum crunch. If made too early, the bread absorbs moisture from the avocado and toppings, becoming soggy and limp, which ruins the bite experience.
Add lemon juice to avocado immediately after mashing.
Lemon juice slows enzymatic browning, which keeps the avocado bright green and appetizing for longer. This simple step is key to preventing unappealing discoloration.
Lightly salt cherry tomatoes before topping.
Salting tomatoes enhances their natural sweetness by drawing out moisture and concentrating flavors through osmosis. This brings a richer, juicier taste to the bites.
Drizzle olive oil over the finished bites.
Olive oil adds a glossy finish and helps carry the flavors of the toppings, making each bite richer and more satisfying. It also adds healthy fats that complement the avocado.
Handle microgreens gently to prevent bruising.
Bruised microgreens turn brown and lose their fresh taste quickly. Gentle handling keeps them vibrant and adds a fresh, peppery crunch that balances the creamy avocado.
Assemble bites just before serving.
Waiting to assemble prevents the toast from becoming soggy and the ingredients from wilting. This keeps the bites fresh, crisp, and visually appealing for your guests.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using underripe or overly soft avocados.

Why it happens Sometimes people grab avocados that look ripe but are too firm or too mushy, assuming any soft fruit will work.

Fix Check ripeness by gently pressing the avocado—perfectly ripe ones yield slightly but aren’t squishy. Use these to ensure creamy texture without watery or bitter flavors.

Toasting bread too early.

Why it happens Prepping ahead is tempting, but toast left out can absorb moisture from the air or toppings, losing crispness.

Fix Toast bread rounds right before spreading avocado to keep crunch. If you must prep early, store toast airtight and assemble last minute.

Skipping lemon juice in avocado mash.

Why it happens Some skip lemon juice to avoid acidity or assume it’s unnecessary.

Fix Add fresh lemon juice immediately after mashing to prevent browning and add brightness. This keeps your bites looking fresh longer.

Overloading toppings causing soggy bites.

Why it happens It’s tempting to pile on, but too many juicy toppings make the bread soggy quickly.

Fix Use moderate amounts of tomatoes and microgreens, spreading evenly to maintain texture and ease of eating.

Not seasoning properly.

Why it happens Some forget to salt or pepper the avocado or toppings, resulting in bland bites.

Fix Season avocado mash and tomatoes lightly with salt and pepper to enhance natural flavors and balance richness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These avocado toast bites are best served fresh. If left at room temperature, cover with plastic wrap and consume within 1-2 hours to avoid sogginess and browning.

Refrigerator

Store avocado mash separately in an airtight container with a plastic wrap pressed on its surface to prevent browning for up to 1 day. Toast can be kept in a sealed bag for 1-2 days but may lose crispness. Assemble just before serving.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended for avocado toast bites as the texture of avocado and toast degrade significantly. If needed, freeze avocado mash alone in an airtight container for up to 1 month and thaw in fridge before use.

Reheating

Reheat toast rounds briefly in a toaster or oven at medium heat to restore crispness before assembling. Avoid microwaving as it will make the toast soggy. Do not reheat avocado mixture.

Make Ahead

You can prepare the avocado mash and chop tomatoes a day ahead, storing them separately in airtight containers. Toast the bread rounds just before serving to keep them crisp. Assemble the bites last minute for best texture and flavor.

FAQ

Can I use regular bread slices instead of rounds?
Yes, you can use regular bread slices, but to keep the appetizer bite-sized and easy to eat, I recommend cutting them into small squares or triangles. Toast them well to maintain crispness and prevent sogginess.
How do I prevent the avocado from browning quickly?
Adding fresh lemon juice to the mashed avocado slows oxidation, which causes browning. Also, store the avocado mash in an airtight container with plastic wrap pressed directly on the surface if you need to prep ahead.
Can I make this recipe vegan?
This recipe is naturally vegan as is, since it uses only plant-based ingredients like avocado, tomatoes, olive oil, and microgreens. Just double-check your bread is free of dairy or eggs if buying pre-made.
What can I substitute for microgreens?
If you don’t have microgreens, baby arugula, fresh basil, or cilantro make good substitutes. They add fresh, peppery, or herbal notes that complement the creamy avocado nicely.
How long can I store the avocado mash?
Stored properly with lemon juice and plastic wrap on the surface, avocado mash can last about 1 day in the fridge. It’s best to prepare it fresh for optimal flavor and color.
Can I add other toppings to these bites?
Absolutely! Feel free to add crumbled cheese, smoked salmon, or a drizzle of balsamic glaze for more flavor variations. Just keep toppings moderate to avoid sogginess.
Is it okay to use bottled lemon juice?
While bottled lemon juice can work in a pinch, fresh lemon juice tastes brighter and has more natural enzymes that help prevent browning. Fresh juice also enhances the flavor better.
Can I prepare these bites for a party in advance?
You can prep components like avocado mash and tomato topping a few hours ahead, but assemble the bites shortly before serving to keep the toast crisp and fresh.
