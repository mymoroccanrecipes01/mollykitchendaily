Sheet Pan Breakfast Pizza Loaded with Sausage, Eggs & Cheese

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on July 18, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This sheet pan breakfast pizza is my favorite for weekend brunches. It’s packed with crispy sausage, melted cheese, and eggs baked right on top for a hearty, crowd-pleasing meal. Perfect for lazy mornings!

Introduction

I first made this sheet pan breakfast pizza years ago when I needed a simple way to feed a big group on a weekend morning. It was a hit right away! What I love about this recipe is how it combines all the best breakfast flavors—crispy sausage, gooey cheese, and eggs baked right on top—in one easy pan. No fuss, no juggling multiple dishes. My secret is using a good quality pizza dough that crisps up nicely in the oven and layering the eggs carefully so they bake perfectly without overcooking. I also sprinkle Parmesan on top for a subtle nutty flavor that takes it up a notch. The finished pizza is golden, cheesy, and packed with savory sausage bites, with eggs that are just set but still creamy. It’s perfect for brunch with friends or a lazy weekend breakfast when you want something hearty but effortless. Honestly, I’ve tried versions with different toppings, but this classic combo always wins. If you love breakfast pizza but hate the mess, this sheet pan method is your new best friend!

What You'll Need

One 12x16 inch sheet pan, serves 6

  • 1 lb breakfast sausage, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 sheet pizza dough (about 12x16 inches), thawed
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). While it’s warming up, roll out the pizza dough on a lightly floured surface to fit your sheet pan. Transfer it to the pan and brush the top lightly with olive oil. This step is crucial because it prevents the dough from getting soggy and helps it crisp up nicely during baking. Make sure the dough is evenly spread without thin spots to avoid uneven cooking.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Dough is evenly coated with oil and fits snugly in the pan without gaps.
Step 2
Cook the breakfast sausage in a skillet over medium heat until browned and crispy, breaking it into small pieces with a spoon. Drain excess fat to keep the pizza from becoming greasy. This step adds flavor and texture, and cooking the sausage fully beforehand ensures it’s safe and flavorful. Don’t rush this; well-cooked sausage gives the best taste and texture.
⏱ Time: 8 minutes
✓ Doneness: Sausage is browned all over and cooked through with no pink remaining.
Step 3
Sprinkle the mozzarella and cheddar cheese evenly over the oiled dough. Layering the cheese before adding the sausage helps it melt into the crust, creating a gooey base that holds the toppings together. Be generous but don’t overload, or the dough might get soggy underneath.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cheese covers the dough in an even layer without clumps.
Step 4
Evenly distribute the cooked sausage over the cheese layer. This arrangement lets the sausage crisp slightly in the oven while melding with the melted cheese beneath. Avoid piling it too thickly in one spot to ensure even cooking and prevent sogginess.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Sausage pieces are spread evenly with no large clumps.
Step 5
Carefully crack the eggs directly onto the pizza, spacing them evenly. This step requires a gentle touch to keep the yolks intact. The eggs bake on top, providing a creamy, rich texture that contrasts perfectly with the crispy sausage and melty cheese. If you prefer, you can scramble the eggs beforehand, but I love the look and texture of baked eggs.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Eggs are spaced evenly with yolks whole and whites intact.
Step 6
Sprinkle the Parmesan cheese and Italian seasoning over the entire pizza, then season with salt and pepper to taste. The Parmesan adds a subtle umami boost, while the herbs provide fresh, aromatic notes that brighten the dish. Don’t skip this step—it really elevates the flavor.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Cheese and seasoning are evenly sprinkled on top.
Step 7
Place the sheet pan in the preheated oven and bake for 15-20 minutes. Keep an eye on the eggs; you want the whites to be set but the yolks still slightly runny, or fully set if you prefer. Baking at high heat crisps the dough edges and melts the cheese beautifully. Avoid opening the oven too often to keep the temperature steady.
⏱ Time: 15-20 minutes
✓ Doneness: Egg whites are opaque and set; crust is golden brown and crispy.
Step 8
Remove the pizza from the oven and let it rest for 5 minutes before slicing. This resting time helps the cheese set slightly, making it easier to cut and preventing toppings from sliding off. Plus, it lets the flavors meld together for a richer taste experience.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pizza is slightly cooled, cheese is set but still melty.
Step 9
Slice the pizza into squares or rectangles and serve warm. This breakfast pizza is great with a side of fresh fruit or a simple green salad for a balanced brunch. Enjoy the combination of crispy, gooey, and creamy textures in every bite!
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Slices hold together well with toppings intact.

Variations

Veggie Lover’s Breakfast Pizza

Swap sausage for sautéed bell peppers, mushrooms, and spinach for a colorful vegetarian version. Add extra mozzarella for creaminess and a sprinkle of red pepper flakes for a kick. It’s lighter but still packed with flavor and texture.

Note: Sauté veggies beforehand to remove moisture and avoid soggy crust; bake eggs as usual.

Spicy Chorizo Breakfast Pizza

Replace breakfast sausage with spicy chorizo for a bold twist. Add sliced jalapeños and a drizzle of hot sauce after baking for extra heat. This variation has a smoky, spicy flavor profile that wakes up your taste buds.

Note: Chorizo renders more fat, so drain well and consider reducing olive oil on dough.

Cheesy Bacon and Egg Breakfast Pizza

Use crispy bacon instead of sausage and double up on cheddar cheese for a smoky, cheesy delight. The bacon adds crunch and saltiness that pairs perfectly with the creamy eggs on top.

Note: Cook bacon until crispy and chop before adding; watch salt levels as bacon is salty.

Gluten-Free Breakfast Pizza

Use a gluten-free pizza crust or flatbread as the base. Follow the same topping and baking steps. This is a great option for gluten-sensitive eaters without sacrificing flavor or texture.

Note: Gluten-free crusts may bake faster; monitor closely to avoid burning.

Herbed Goat Cheese and Veggie Pizza

Swap mozzarella for dollops of creamy goat cheese and add fresh herbs like thyme and basil. Add roasted cherry tomatoes and zucchini for a fresh, tangy flavor with a soft, creamy texture.

Note: Add goat cheese after baking to prevent overcooking; roast veggies beforehand.

Pro Tips

Roll the dough evenly and don’t stretch too thin.
An even dough thickness ensures consistent cooking—thin spots can burn while thick areas remain doughy. Stretching too thin also risks tearing, which lets toppings leak and causes sogginess.
Cook and drain the sausage before topping the pizza.
Pre-cooking renders out fat and crisps the sausage, adding flavor and preventing greasy pizza. Draining excess fat stops the crust from becoming soggy and helps toppings stay flavorful but balanced.
Use a mix of mozzarella and cheddar cheese.
Mozzarella melts smoothly and gives stretch, while cheddar adds sharpness and color. This combination balances creaminess and flavor complexity, making the pizza more interesting.
Crack eggs gently onto the pizza and space them out.
Keeping yolks intact creates a creamy richness and visual appeal. Spacing eggs evenly ensures they cook uniformly without merging or overcooking.
Bake at high heat (425°F) for a crispy crust and perfectly cooked eggs.
High heat crisps the dough edges quickly and melts cheese without drying out eggs. Lower temperatures can cause soggy crust or rubbery eggs.
Let the pizza rest a few minutes before slicing.
Resting allows cheese to set slightly, preventing toppings from sliding off and making slices cleaner and easier to serve.
Use fresh Parmesan for topping.
Freshly grated Parmesan adds a sharper, nuttier flavor and helps create a golden, flavorful crust, unlike pre-grated powders which can be bland.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping pre-cooking the sausage.

Why it happens People often want to save time or think sausage will cook fully on the pizza, but raw sausage releases too much fat and stays undercooked.

Fix Always brown the sausage fully in a skillet and drain excess fat before adding it to the pizza. This ensures safety and flavor without sogginess.

Using dough straight from the fridge without thawing.

Why it happens Impatience or forgetting to plan ahead leads to cold, stiff dough that tears easily and won’t stretch well.

Fix Allow dough to come to room temperature for 30-60 minutes before rolling. This makes it more elastic and easier to work with.

Overcrowding the pizza with toppings.

Why it happens Trying to add too much sausage or cheese can weigh down the crust and prevent proper cooking.

Fix Spread toppings evenly and moderately; this helps the crust stay crisp and toppings cook evenly.

Baking at too low a temperature.

Why it happens Some assume lower temps prevent burning, but it actually causes soggy crust and rubbery eggs.

Fix Bake at 425°F for optimal crust crispness and perfectly set eggs.

Breaking yolks when cracking eggs onto pizza.

Why it happens Rushing or cracking eggs too close to the pizza surface causes yolks to break, losing texture and appearance.

Fix Crack eggs into a small bowl first, then gently slide onto pizza, spacing them well.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This breakfast pizza can safely sit at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered loosely with foil or a clean kitchen towel to prevent drying out.

Refrigerator

Store leftovers in an airtight container or wrapped tightly in plastic wrap for up to 3 days. The crust may soften when cold, so I recommend reheating before serving to restore crispness.

Freezer

Wrap slices individually in plastic wrap and foil to prevent freezer burn, then freeze for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating to maintain texture.

Reheating

Reheat slices in a preheated oven at 350°F for 8-10 minutes until warmed through and crust is crisp again. Avoid microwave reheating as it makes the crust soggy. Add a splash of water to a pan and cover if reheating stovetop to keep eggs moist.

Make Ahead

You can prepare the dough, cook sausage, and shred cheese 1-2 days ahead. Assemble the pizza and store covered in the fridge, then bake fresh on the day of serving for best texture and flavor.

FAQ

Can I use store-bought pizza sauce on this breakfast pizza?
This recipe doesn’t call for tomato sauce because the focus is on sausage, eggs, and cheese flavors. Adding sauce can make the crust soggy. If you want sauce, spread a very thin layer and bake immediately to avoid sogginess.
How do I keep the eggs from overcooking or breaking?
Crack eggs gently onto the pizza, spacing them apart. Bake at high heat and watch closely—eggs cook quickly and whites should be set but yolks still creamy. Opening the oven too often lets heat escape and can cause uneven cooking.
Can I make this pizza vegetarian?
Absolutely! Swap sausage for sautéed mushrooms, spinach, peppers, or your favorite veggies. Pre-cook them to reduce moisture and maintain crust crispness.
What if I don’t have pizza dough?
You can use puff pastry or flatbread as a base, but baking times and textures will vary. Puff pastry will be flakier, and flatbread thinner and crispier.
Can I prepare this the night before?
You can assemble the pizza without eggs and store it covered in the fridge overnight. Add eggs and bake fresh in the morning for best results.
How do I reheat leftovers without losing crispiness?
Reheat in a 350°F oven for 8-10 minutes uncovered. Avoid microwaving as it makes the crust soggy.
Is it possible to freeze the whole pizza?
Freezing the whole assembled pizza with raw eggs is not recommended. Instead, freeze cooked slices or assemble without eggs and freeze, then add eggs and bake fresh.
Can I use different cheeses?
Yes! Mozzarella and cheddar work best for melt and flavor balance, but feel free to try provolone, fontina, or gouda for different tastes.
