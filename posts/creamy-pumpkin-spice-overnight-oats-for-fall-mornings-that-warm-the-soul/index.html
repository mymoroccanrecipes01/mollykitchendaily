Cozy Pumpkin Spice Overnight Oats for Busy Fall Mornings

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 4, 2026
15 min Prep Time
2 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This pumpkin spice overnight oats recipe is my go-to for chilly mornings. Soaked overnight for creamy texture, it’s packed with cozy fall flavors and ready as soon as you wake up—perfect for busy days!

Introduction

I first stumbled on this pumpkin spice overnight oats recipe a couple of years ago when I wanted something cozy and fuss-free for fall mornings. Honestly, I’ve tried many versions, but this one quickly became a favorite because it’s so simple yet packed with that warm pumpkin flavor I adore. The trick I learned was soaking the oats overnight with pumpkin puree and spices—this not only softens the oats perfectly but also infuses them with cozy, autumn vibes. I used to struggle with oats that turned out too mushy or bland, but this balance is just right—creamy, slightly sweet, and lightly spiced. Plus, it's ready as soon as I open the fridge, so it’s perfect for those rushed mornings when I still want something healthy and satisfying. The texture is silky, and the pumpkin flavor really shines without being overpowering. I like topping mine with a sprinkle of cinnamon or some chopped nuts for crunch. If you love pumpkin spice as much as I do, this recipe is going to be your new morning best friend!

What You'll Need

2 servings

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats, old-fashioned
  • 1/2 cup milk of choice, cold
  • 1/4 cup pumpkin puree, canned or fresh
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup or honey
  • 1/2 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

How To Do It

Step 1
In a medium bowl or jar, combine the rolled oats, chia seeds, and a pinch of salt. Stir these dry ingredients together so that the chia seeds are evenly distributed. This step is important because chia seeds help thicken the oats as they soak, giving you that creamy overnight oats texture without needing to cook. Make sure the oats are old-fashioned rolled oats and not instant or steel-cut, as those have very different textures and soaking times.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Dry ingredients are evenly mixed with no clumps of chia seeds.
Step 2
Add the pumpkin puree, milk, maple syrup, pumpkin pie spice, and vanilla extract to the oat mixture. Stir everything until well combined. The pumpkin puree adds moisture and flavor while the spices bring that classic fall taste. If you skip mixing well here, the spices might clump or the texture won’t be uniform. This mixture should be smooth and creamy, not lumpy. Using cold milk helps keep the oats chilled and fresh overnight.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is smooth and evenly colored with no dry spots.
Step 3
Cover your bowl or jar tightly with a lid or plastic wrap to prevent the oats from absorbing any fridge odors. Place it in the refrigerator to soak overnight, or at least for 6 hours. This soaking time is critical as it softens the oats and allows the flavors to meld. Rushing this step will leave the oats tough and the texture less creamy. Overnight is best, but 6 hours is the minimum for fully softened oats.
⏱ Time: 6+ hours (overnight)
✓ Doneness: Oats are swollen, soft, and creamy when checked in the morning.
Step 4
In the morning, give your oats a good stir to redistribute any settled ingredients and check the consistency. If it’s too thick, add a splash of milk and stir again. This helps loosen the oats to your preferred creaminess. Don’t add too much liquid at once—better to add little by little so you don’t make it watery. Adjust sweetness or spice if you like by stirring in a bit more maple syrup or cinnamon.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Oats are creamy but not watery, with a smooth texture.
Step 5
Top your pumpkin spice overnight oats with your favorite toppings like chopped nuts, seeds, fresh fruit, or a dollop of yogurt. This adds texture and flavor contrast, making your breakfast more interesting and satisfying. I love walnuts or pecans here for a nice crunch that complements the soft oats. Don’t skip toppings—they elevate the dish and add nutrition.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Toppings are evenly scattered and visually appealing.
Step 6
If you want to prepare ahead for the week, multiply the ingredients and portion the oats into individual jars. This makes grab-and-go breakfasts super easy. Store covered in the fridge and add toppings fresh each morning for best texture. Just remember that the oats may absorb liquid over a few days, so stir in a little milk before eating if needed.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Portions are evenly divided in jars, ready for storage.
Step 7
For a dairy-free version, swap regular milk for almond, oat, or coconut milk. These plant-based milks work beautifully with pumpkin and spices, adding their own subtle flavors. Just be sure to use unsweetened varieties if you want to control sugar levels. The soaking time and texture remain the same.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Milk substitution fully incorporated without clumps.
Step 8
If you want a thicker texture, increase chia seeds to 1.5 tablespoons, but be careful not to add too much or the oats can get gelatinous. Chia seeds absorb liquid and swell, so this controls how creamy or thick your oats become. Experiment with this ratio to find your perfect texture.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Mixture is thicker but still smooth and spoonable.
Step 9
Experiment with spices! You can add a pinch of ground cinnamon, nutmeg, or ginger along with pumpkin pie spice for a more complex flavor. Just remember to start small and taste the mixture before soaking to avoid overpowering the oats. The spices infuse during soaking and mellow out, so balance is key.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Spices are evenly mixed with no clumps or overpowering aroma.

Variations

Vegan Pumpkin Spice Overnight Oats

Swap dairy milk for almond or oat milk and use maple syrup instead of honey to make this recipe fully vegan. The plant-based milk adds a subtle nutty flavor that complements the pumpkin perfectly. You’ll still get creamy oats with that classic fall taste—just dairy-free!

Note: Soaking times remain the same. Choose unsweetened plant milk to avoid extra sweetness and maintain flavor balance.

Spiced Pumpkin and Apple Overnight Oats

Add finely chopped apples and a pinch of cinnamon to the mix for a fresh twist. The apple adds natural sweetness and texture, while the extra cinnamon boosts warmth. This version feels like a mini apple pie in a jar and is great for autumn breakfasts.

Note: Chop apples small to soften during soaking. You may want to reduce maple syrup slightly due to apple sweetness.

Pumpkin Spice Protein Overnight Oats

Boost the protein by stirring in your favorite protein powder or Greek yogurt before soaking. This makes the oats more filling and perfect for post-workout or a longer-lasting breakfast. The added protein blends well with pumpkin and spices.

Note: Use unflavored or vanilla protein powder to avoid clashing flavors. Adjust liquid slightly if mixture becomes too thick.

Pumpkin Spice Overnight Oats with Toasted Pecans

Top your oats with toasted pecans and a drizzle of maple syrup for a crunchy, sweet finish. Toasting nuts enhances their flavor and adds a toasty note that pairs wonderfully with pumpkin and spices. This is my favorite way to enjoy these oats when I want a little extra indulgence.

Note: Toast pecans in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3-5 minutes until fragrant. Add toppings just before serving.

Chocolate Pumpkin Spice Overnight Oats

Stir in a tablespoon of cocoa powder and sprinkle chocolate chips on top for a decadent twist. The chocolate pairs surprisingly well with the pumpkin spice, creating a rich, dessert-like breakfast that’s still wholesome and easy to make.

Note: Use unsweetened cocoa powder and adjust sweetener to taste. Add chocolate chips just before serving to prevent melting.

Pro Tips

Use old-fashioned rolled oats instead of instant oats for the best texture.
Old-fashioned oats soak up liquid without turning mushy, giving a nice chewy but creamy texture. Instant oats absorb liquid too quickly and become overly soft, losing the satisfying bite that makes overnight oats enjoyable.
Add chia seeds to thicken the oats naturally.
Chia seeds absorb moisture and swell, creating a gel that thickens the mixture without cooking. This keeps the oats creamy and stable, preventing them from separating or becoming watery after soaking.
Let the oats soak for at least 6 hours or overnight for full flavor and texture development.
Soaking time allows the oats to soften and the spices to infuse fully. Rushing this step results in tough oats and muted flavors, defeating the purpose of overnight oats.
Use pure pumpkin puree, not pumpkin pie filling.
Pumpkin pie filling contains added sugar and spices that can overpower or unbalance the recipe. Pure pumpkin puree gives you control over sweetness and spice levels, ensuring a fresh, natural pumpkin flavor.
Stir the oats well before refrigerating to evenly distribute spices and pumpkin.
Even mixing prevents clumps of spices or pumpkin and ensures every bite is flavorful and consistent. Uneven mixing can lead to bland spots or overly spiced areas.
Adjust liquid after soaking if oats are too thick.
Oats absorb liquid differently depending on the brand and freshness. Adding a splash of milk after soaking lets you customize the creaminess and prevents the oats from feeling dry or dense.
Top with crunchy nuts or seeds for texture contrast.
The soft, creamy oats benefit from a crunchy topping like walnuts or pecans. This adds interest and makes the breakfast more satisfying, balancing textures and flavors.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using instant oats instead of rolled oats

Why it happens People often grab whatever oats are on hand without realizing the difference, assuming all oats behave the same.

Fix Use old-fashioned rolled oats specifically for overnight oats. They soak up liquid properly and retain a pleasant texture. Instant oats become mushy and lose the signature overnight oats texture.

Not soaking long enough

Why it happens Impatience or forgetting to prepare the night before leads to insufficient soaking time.

Fix Plan ahead and soak the oats at least 6 hours or overnight. This softens the oats and allows flavors to meld, making the dish creamy and flavorful.

Skipping chia seeds

Why it happens Some people omit chia seeds thinking they’re optional or don’t like the texture.

Fix Chia seeds are key for thickening and texture. If you must omit, increase oats slightly and reduce liquid to compensate, but expect a looser texture.

Using pumpkin pie filling instead of pure pumpkin

Why it happens Confusing canned pumpkin pie filling with plain pumpkin puree is common.

Fix Always use unsweetened pumpkin puree to control sugar and spice levels. Pumpkin pie filling alters flavor and sweetness unpredictably.

Not mixing ingredients well before soaking

Why it happens Rushing or layering ingredients without stirring can leave spices clumped or pumpkin unevenly distributed.

Fix Mix thoroughly before refrigerating to ensure even flavor and texture throughout the oats.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Overnight oats should not be left at room temperature for more than 2 hours to prevent bacterial growth. Always keep them refrigerated until ready to eat.

Refrigerator

Store overnight oats in an airtight container or jar for up to 3 days. The texture may thicken over time; stir in a splash of milk before eating to loosen. Flavors may deepen but fresh toppings are best added just before serving.

Freezer

Freezing overnight oats is not recommended as the texture can become grainy when thawed. If you must freeze, portion into airtight containers for up to 1 month and thaw overnight in the fridge before eating, stirring well to recombine.

Reheating

Overnight oats are typically eaten cold, but you can warm them gently in the microwave for 30-60 seconds if desired. Add a splash of milk before heating to maintain creaminess. Avoid overheating as it can change the texture.

Make Ahead

Prepare the oat mixture 1-2 days ahead and store in the fridge. Add toppings fresh each morning. This saves time while keeping the oats fresh and flavorful. For busy weeks, portion into jars for easy grab-and-go breakfasts.

FAQ

Can I use steel-cut oats instead of rolled oats?
Steel-cut oats require longer cooking and do not soften well just by soaking overnight. They remain chewy and hard if not cooked. For overnight oats, stick to old-fashioned rolled oats for the best texture.
How can I make this recipe sweeter without adding sugar?
You can add naturally sweet toppings like fresh or dried fruit, a splash of vanilla extract, or a little more maple syrup or honey if you prefer. Using ripe bananas mashed into the oats also adds natural sweetness.
Is it okay to prepare this recipe in larger batches?
Absolutely! You can multiply the ingredients and portion into individual jars for the week. Just remember to stir well before eating and add fresh toppings daily for best taste and texture.
What if my overnight oats are too thick or too thin?
Adjust the milk quantity to reach your preferred consistency. If too thick, stir in more milk before eating. If too thin, add a bit more oats or chia seeds next time and allow longer soaking.
Can I add protein powder to this recipe?
Yes, adding protein powder or Greek yogurt is a great way to boost protein content. Use unflavored or vanilla powder to complement the spices, and adjust liquid amounts to maintain creamy texture.
How long can I store overnight oats in the fridge?
Overnight oats can be stored safely in the fridge for up to 3 days. Beyond that, the texture and flavor may degrade. For best quality, consume within this timeframe.
Can I eat these oats warm instead of cold?
Yes! Although they’re traditionally eaten cold, you can warm them in the microwave for 30-60 seconds. Add a splash of milk before heating to keep them creamy. Avoid overheating to prevent a gummy texture.
What toppings do you recommend for pumpkin spice overnight oats?
I love crunchy nuts like pecans or walnuts, fresh fruit such as sliced apples or berries, a dollop of yogurt, or a sprinkle of cinnamon. These add texture and enhance flavor.
