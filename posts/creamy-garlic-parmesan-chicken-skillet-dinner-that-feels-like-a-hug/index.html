Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Skillet Dinner for Cozy Weeknights

Molly Kitchen Daily Published on May 29, 2026
15 min prep 30 min cook 4 servings

This skillet chicken is comfort food at its best—juicy chicken breasts in a garlicky, creamy Parmesan sauce. The splash of cream and fresh Parmesan takes it over the top. Perfect for busy, chilly evenings!

Why This Works

What makes this recipe stand out is the balance between rich creaminess and bright, savory garlic and Parmesan flavors. Searing the chicken first creates a Maillard reaction, which is a chemical process where proteins and sugars react under high heat to form complex flavors and a browned crust. This crust not only tastes great but helps lock in moisture, keeping the chicken juicy throughout cooking.

Deglazing the pan with chicken broth lifts those flavorful browned bits, infusing the sauce with depth. The gentle simmering of cream and Parmesan creates a luscious emulsion. Parmesan cheese contains proteins and fats that help thicken the sauce as it melts, while the cream adds silkiness and richness without overpowering the other ingredients.

Temperature control is key: cooking garlic briefly prevents bitterness, and keeping the cream at a gentle simmer stops it from curdling. The Italian herbs add an aromatic complexity that complements the savory sauce without overwhelming it. Finally, resting the chicken in the sauce enhances the flavor absorption and improves texture, making each bite tender and flavorful.

Overall, this recipe’s success lies in layering flavors carefully and using cooking techniques that maximize moisture retention and sauce texture. It’s a simple yet effective way to elevate a weeknight chicken dinner into something special.

  • Choose fresh, boneless, skinless chicken breasts that are plump and pale pink with no discoloration. Thicker breasts work well here because they stay juicy when seared properly. Avoid frozen or previously frozen chicken for best texture. Pat them dry before seasoning to ensure a good sear and prevent steaming.
  • Use heavy cream with at least 36% fat content for a rich, silky sauce. Lower-fat creams or milk won’t provide the same luscious texture and may curdle when simmered. Heavy cream emulsifies with the cheese to create a stable, creamy sauce that clings well to the chicken.
  • Freshly grated Parmesan is essential for this recipe. It melts smoothly and adds a sharp, nutty flavor. Avoid pre-grated Parmesan which often contains anti-caking agents that can prevent proper melting and cause a grainy texture. Look for Parmigiano-Reggiano for the best flavor.
  • Fresh garlic cloves provide a bright, aromatic punch. Mince finely to release maximum flavor. Avoid pre-minced garlic from jars, which can be less fresh and sometimes bitter. Cook garlic briefly over moderate heat to avoid burning, which turns it bitter and unpleasant.
  • Use low-sodium chicken broth to control the saltiness of the dish. It adds moisture and depth without overpowering the subtle flavors. The broth helps deglaze the pan and dissolve the flavorful browned bits left from searing the chicken, enriching the sauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts, patted dry
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • Fresh parsley, chopped for garnish

How To Do It

  1. Step 1

    Season the chicken breasts generously on both sides with salt and freshly ground black pepper. This step is crucial because well-seasoned chicken forms the foundation of flavor for the entire dish. When you season before cooking, the salt helps to enhance the natural taste and also aids in creating a nice crust during searing. Avoid under-seasoning here, as it’s harder to fix later. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering but not smoking.

    ⏱ 5 minutes✓ Chicken surface is golden brown and releases easily from the pan
  2. Step 2

    Add the chicken breasts to the hot skillet and sear without moving for about 5-6 minutes until the bottom is golden and forms a crust. Flip and sear the other side for another 5 minutes. This searing step locks in juices and creates a flavorful crust through the Maillard reaction, which develops complex flavors and appealing color. Don’t rush this; moving the chicken too soon will cause sticking and uneven browning.

    ⏱ 10-12 minutes✓ Both sides are golden brown and chicken releases easily when flipped
  3. Step 3

    Remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside on a plate. Lower the heat to medium and add the minced garlic to the same pan. Sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant but not browned. Garlic burns quickly and becomes bitter, so watch closely and stir constantly. Cooking garlic in the flavorful browned bits left from the chicken adds depth to the sauce.

    ⏱ 1 minute✓ Garlic is fragrant and slightly softened but not browned
  4. Step 4

    Pour in the chicken broth and use a wooden spoon to scrape up all the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. These bits are packed with flavor and will enrich your sauce. Let the broth simmer for 2-3 minutes to reduce slightly. This step intensifies the taste and ensures your sauce won’t be watery.

    ⏱ 3 minutes✓ Broth slightly reduced and pan deglazed with no stuck bits
  5. Step 5

    Stir in the heavy cream and dried Italian herbs. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, stirring occasionally. The cream adds luscious richness, balancing the garlic’s sharpness and Parmesan’s saltiness. Keep the heat moderate to prevent the cream from curdling. If it simmers too vigorously, the texture will suffer.

    ⏱ 3 minutes✓ Sauce slightly thickened and bubbling gently
  6. Step 6

    Add the freshly grated Parmesan cheese gradually while stirring the sauce. Freshly grated cheese melts better and creates a smooth, creamy texture. Avoid pre-grated cheese as it often contains anti-caking agents that can affect melting. Stir until the cheese is fully incorporated and the sauce thickens to coat the back of a spoon.

    ⏱ 3-4 minutes✓ Sauce is creamy, smooth, and thick enough to coat chicken
  7. Step 7

    Return the chicken breasts to the skillet, nestling them into the sauce. Spoon some sauce over the tops. Cover the pan loosely and let everything simmer gently for 8-10 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 165°F). This gentle simmer finishes cooking the chicken and allows it to soak up the flavors of the sauce.

    ⏱ 10 minutes✓ Chicken is opaque and juices run clear when pierced
  8. Step 8

    Remove the lid and increase heat to medium-high to reduce the sauce slightly if it seems too thin. Stir occasionally and watch carefully so it doesn’t burn. The sauce should cling nicely to the chicken without being runny.

    ⏱ 3-4 minutes✓ Sauce coats the back of a spoon and thickens slightly
  9. Step 9

    Turn off the heat and sprinkle chopped fresh parsley over the chicken for a pop of color and fresh flavor. Let the dish rest for a couple of minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld. This finishing touch brightens the rich sauce and makes the presentation inviting.

    ⏱ 2 minutes✓ Dish looks vibrant and aromatic with fresh herbs

Variations

  • Spinach and Mushroom Creamy Chicken: Add fresh spinach and sliced mushrooms to the skillet after sautéing garlic. Sauté until mushrooms soften and spinach wilts before adding broth and cream. This adds earthiness and a veggie boost without changing the creamy texture.
    Cook mushrooms until golden to avoid excess water in the sauce and wilt spinach just before adding liquids to prevent overcooking.
  • Spicy Creamy Parmesan Chicken: Add crushed red pepper flakes or a dash of cayenne pepper to the garlic step for a spicy kick. This variation keeps the creamy base but adds warmth and depth, perfect for those who like a little heat.
    Start with a small pinch of spice and adjust to taste to avoid overpowering the garlic and cheese flavors.
  • Lemon Herb Parmesan Chicken: Stir in fresh lemon juice and zest along with the cream for a bright, tangy twist. Fresh herbs like thyme or basil can replace dried herbs for a fresher flavor profile. This lightens the richness and adds freshness.
    Add lemon juice at the end of simmering to preserve its brightness and avoid bitterness.
  • Low-Carb Creamy Parmesan Chicken: Serve the creamy chicken over cauliflower rice or steamed veggies instead of pasta or bread. This keeps the meal low-carb while maintaining all the rich flavors of the sauce.
    Avoid adding flour or starch to the sauce to keep it naturally thickened by cheese and cream.
  • Creamy Parmesan Chicken with Sun-Dried Tomatoes: Add chopped sun-dried tomatoes to the garlic and sauté briefly before adding broth. This adds a sweet, tangy depth to the sauce that complements the creamy Parmesan beautifully.
    Use oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes for more flavor and drain excess oil to prevent the sauce from becoming greasy.

Pro Tips

  • Pat the chicken dry before seasoning. — Removing excess moisture ensures the chicken sears properly instead of steaming, which helps develop a flavorful crust. If the chicken is wet, it won’t brown well and the texture will be less appealing.
  • Use freshly grated Parmesan cheese. — Freshly grated cheese melts smoothly into the sauce, creating a creamy texture and rich flavor. Pre-grated cheese often contains additives that can result in a grainy sauce.
  • Don’t rush cooking the garlic. — Garlic burns quickly and turns bitter if cooked too long or on high heat. Cook it just until fragrant over medium heat to keep the flavor bright and pleasant.
  • Simmer the sauce gently. — High heat can cause the cream to curdle or the sauce to scorch. A gentle simmer ensures the sauce thickens evenly and stays smooth.
  • Let the chicken rest in the sauce before serving. — Resting allows the chicken to absorb the sauce flavors and finish cooking evenly, resulting in juicier, more tender meat.
  • Use a heavy-bottomed skillet. — A heavy pan distributes heat evenly, preventing hot spots that can burn the sauce or chicken. It helps maintain steady cooking temperatures.
  • Deglaze the pan with broth before adding cream. — This lifts flavorful browned bits from the pan and prevents the cream from burning when added directly. It builds a deeper, more complex sauce.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the sear on the chicken.
    Why: Some cooks rush to add the sauce and skip searing, thinking it saves time.
    Fix: Always sear chicken first to develop flavor and lock in moisture. Make sure the pan and oil are hot before adding chicken and don’t move it too soon.
  • Burning the garlic.
    Why: Garlic cooks quickly and can burn if left unattended or cooked on too high heat.
    Fix: Add garlic after searing the chicken, cook on medium heat, and stir constantly for just 30 seconds to 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Using pre-grated Parmesan cheese.
    Why: Convenience leads some to use pre-grated cheese, unaware it affects texture.
    Fix: Grate fresh Parmesan yourself for smooth melting and better flavor.
  • Boiling the cream sauce.
    Why: High heat can cause cream to curdle and sauce to separate.
    Fix: Simmer sauce gently over medium or medium-low heat, stirring often to keep it smooth.
  • Not letting the chicken rest in the sauce.
    Why: Eager to serve, some remove chicken immediately after cooking.
    Fix: Allow chicken to simmer in the sauce for 5-10 minutes off heat to absorb flavor and finish cooking.

Storage & Tips

  • 🌡️ Room Temperature: This dish should not be left at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Cover loosely if you need to keep it out briefly, but refrigerate promptly to maintain food safety and freshness.
  • ❄️ Refrigerator: Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. The sauce may thicken upon chilling; gently reheat on the stovetop with a splash of broth or cream to loosen it. The chicken remains tender but avoid overcooking during reheating.
  • 🧊 Freezer: Freeze in a sealed container for up to 2 months. To prevent freezer burn, wrap the container with extra foil or place inside a freezer bag. Thaw in the fridge overnight before reheating gently on the stove with added liquid to restore sauce consistency.
  • ♨️ Reheating: Reheat over low heat in a covered skillet, stirring occasionally. Add a splash of chicken broth or cream if the sauce is too thick. Avoid microwaving at high power as it can dry out the chicken and curdle the sauce.
  • 📅 Make Ahead: You can sear the chicken and prepare the sauce up to 1 day ahead. Store chicken and sauce separately in the fridge. On serving day, combine and gently reheat, then finish with fresh parsley. This saves time and keeps the chicken juicy.

FAQ

Can I use chicken thighs instead of breasts?
Absolutely! Boneless, skinless chicken thighs work great and tend to stay even juicier. Just adjust the cooking time slightly as thighs may take a bit longer to cook through. The richer flavor of thighs pairs nicely with the creamy sauce.
What if I don’t have heavy cream?
You can substitute with half-and-half or whole milk mixed with a bit of butter for richness. However, the sauce will be less thick and creamy. To help thicken, you can add a small amount of cornstarch slurry, but be careful not to over-thicken.
How do I prevent the cream from curdling?
Keep the heat moderate and avoid boiling the sauce vigorously. Simmer gently and stir often. Adding cream after deglazing with broth also helps temper the temperature and prevents curdling.
Can I prepare this dish gluten-free?
Yes! This recipe is naturally gluten-free as written since it uses no flour or wheat-based thickeners. Just double-check your broth and Parmesan cheese labels to ensure they don’t contain any gluten additives.
Is it okay to use pre-minced garlic?
Fresh garlic always tastes best, but pre-minced can work in a pinch. Just be aware it may have a slightly different flavor and won’t be as bright. Cook it gently to avoid bitterness.
Can I make this recipe dairy-free?
You’d need to substitute heavy cream with coconut cream or a non-dairy creamer and use a dairy-free Parmesan alternative. The flavor and texture will differ, but it can still be tasty with those swaps.
How should I store leftovers?
Keep leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stove with a splash of broth or cream to restore sauce texture. Avoid reheating multiple times to maintain quality.
What side dishes pair well with this chicken?
This creamy chicken goes well with pasta, mashed potatoes, steamed veggies, or a simple green salad. The sauce is rich, so lighter sides balance the meal nicely.
