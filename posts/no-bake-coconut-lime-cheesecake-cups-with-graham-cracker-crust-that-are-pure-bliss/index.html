Refreshing No-Bake Coconut Lime Cheesecake Cups Perfect for Summer

Molly Kitchen Daily Published on May 29, 2026
15 min prep 0 min cook 6 servings

These no-bake cheesecake cups are creamy, tangy, and super easy to make. The combo of coconut and lime gives a fresh twist, while the graham cracker crust adds crunch. Great for warm evenings or last-minute dessert cravings!

Why This Works

This no-bake cheesecake recipe succeeds because of the careful balance between texture and flavor without the use of an oven. The graham cracker crust relies on melted butter to bind the crumbs, creating a firm base that contrasts nicely with the creamy filling. Pressing the crust firmly ensures it holds together when you bite into it, which is essential since it’s not baked to solidify. Without enough butter or pressure, the crust can crumble and fall apart.

The filling’s magic comes from using cream cheese blended thoroughly with coconut cream. Coconut cream adds tropical richness and a slightly sweet undertone, making the cheesecake feel indulgent yet fresh. The acidity from fresh lime juice brightens the flavor and helps balance the richness, preventing it from feeling too heavy. Beating the cream cheese and sugar well is key to achieving a smooth, airy texture that sets up nicely in the fridge without baking.

The chilling step is crucial as it allows the filling to firm up naturally. This slow setting helps the flavors meld and the texture become pleasantly dense yet creamy. Optional gelatin can be used for a firmer set but may alter the mouthfeel. Finally, the toasted coconut garnish adds a crunchy, nutty contrast and intensifies the coconut flavor, making this dessert a perfect balance of creamy, crunchy, tangy, and sweet.

  • Choose finely crushed graham crackers with a slightly sweet flavor and a hint of cinnamon if possible. The crumb size matters because finer crumbs pack better and create a more cohesive crust. Avoid coarse crumbs or overly dry crackers, as they won’t bind well with butter and may result in a crumbly crust.
  • Use unsalted butter so you can control the salt level in the crust. Melt it gently to avoid browning, which can change the flavor. Butter acts as the binding agent for the crumbs, so the right amount is crucial—too little and the crust won’t hold, too much and it becomes greasy.
  • Softened cream cheese is the foundation for the creamy filling. Full-fat cream cheese gives the best texture and richness. Make sure it’s at room temperature to avoid lumps and to blend smoothly with other ingredients. Low-fat versions can result in a less creamy, more rubbery texture.
  • Look for full-fat coconut cream, not coconut milk, which is thinner. Coconut cream adds body and a subtle tropical sweetness that pairs beautifully with lime. Chill it well before use to help it whip up silky. Avoid brands with additives or stabilizers that may affect texture.
  • Freshly squeezed lime juice is essential for the bright, tangy flavor that cuts through the richness of the cream and coconut. Bottled lime juice lacks the brightness and can make the filling taste flat. The acidity also helps the filling set slightly by reacting with the dairy proteins.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs, finely crushed
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup coconut cream
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Zest of 2 limes
  • 1/3 cup fresh lime juice
  • Toasted shredded coconut, for garnish (optional)
  • Fresh lime slices, for garnish (optional)

Introduction

I stumbled upon this no-bake coconut lime cheesecake cups recipe during a particularly hot summer when I wanted something cool and refreshing but didn’t want to heat up the kitchen. The mix of creamy coconut and bright lime zest instantly won me over. What sets this version apart is the use of coconut cream instead of heavy cream, which adds a tropical richness without being too heavy. Plus, I love that the graham cracker crust is quick to make and gives just the right crunch. The trick I learned is to soften the cream cheese well and beat it thoroughly with powdered sugar and lime juice — this gives a smooth, tangy filling that holds up nicely in cups without baking. The finished dessert looks so inviting with a sprinkle of toasted coconut and a lime slice on top, making it perfect for summer evenings or casual get-togethers. Honestly, sometimes I make a double batch because it disappears so fast among family and friends!

How To Do It

  1. Step 1

    Start by making the crust. Mix the graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and granulated sugar in a bowl until evenly combined. The butter binds the crumbs together so they form a firm crust when chilled. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of your serving cups or jars. Use the back of a spoon or a flat-bottomed glass to compact it tightly; this prevents the crust from crumbling when you eat. Avoid pressing too lightly or the crust won’t hold its shape. Chill the crusted cups in the fridge while you prepare the filling to help it set up.

    ⏱ 10✓ Crust feels compact and holds shape when gently pressed
  2. Step 2

    In a mixing bowl, beat the softened cream cheese until smooth and creamy using a hand mixer or whisk. This step is crucial because lumps will ruin the texture. Add the powdered sugar gradually and keep beating until fully incorporated and silky. Then stir in the coconut cream, vanilla extract, lime zest, and fresh lime juice. The acidity from the lime juice helps balance the richness of the cream cheese and coconut cream, giving the filling a bright, tangy flavor. Don’t rush this step; mixing thoroughly ensures a smooth, fluffy consistency.

    ⏱ 10✓ Filling is smooth, glossy, and holds soft peaks when lifted
  3. Step 3

    Spoon or pipe the cheesecake filling over the chilled crust in each cup. Fill them evenly and level the top with the back of a spoon. The filling should be thick enough to hold its shape but still creamy. If it feels too soft, chill it a bit longer before serving. This layering creates the classic no-bake cheesecake structure – a crunchy base with a luscious creamy top. Cover the cups with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or preferably overnight, to let the flavors meld and the filling firm up.

    ⏱ 5✓ Filling is chilled and holds shape without wobbling
  4. Step 4

    Before serving, garnish each cheesecake cup with a sprinkle of toasted shredded coconut and a thin slice of fresh lime. Toast the coconut in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring often, until golden and fragrant. This adds a lovely nutty crunch and enhances the coconut flavor. The lime slice adds a fresh, zesty aroma and looks pretty. This finishing touch makes the dessert look festive and inviting—perfect for impressing guests or just treating yourself.

    ⏱ 5✓ Coconut is golden brown and lime slices are fresh
  5. Step 5

    Serve the cheesecake cups chilled straight from the fridge. They’re best enjoyed cold to keep that creamy texture and refreshing tang. If you leave them out too long, the filling can soften and lose firmness. These cups are perfect for summer parties, picnics, or whenever you want a quick, fuss-free dessert that feels indulgent but light. Plus, no baking means less heat and less hassle!

    ✓ Dessert is cool and refreshing
  6. Step 6

    If you want to transport the cheesecake cups, keep them in a cooler with ice packs to maintain their chilled texture. The crust can soften if left at room temperature too long, so keeping them cool preserves the crunch and creaminess. This makes them ideal for potlucks or outdoor events where refrigeration may be limited. Just be sure to serve them within a few hours of removal from the fridge for best results.

    ✓ Cups remain firm and crust stays crunchy
  7. Step 7

    If you prefer a firmer texture, you can add 1 tablespoon of powdered gelatin dissolved in 2 tablespoons warm water to the filling before chilling. This helps the cheesecake set more firmly, especially if coconut cream is very soft or the room is warm. However, gelatin changes the mouthfeel slightly, making it less creamy and more mousse-like. So, this is optional depending on your texture preference.

    ⏱ 5✓ Filling sets more firmly without weeping or separating
  8. Step 8

    For a dairy-free version, swap the cream cheese with a vegan cream cheese alternative and ensure the coconut cream is full-fat and well chilled. The texture will be slightly different but still creamy and delicious. Beat the vegan cream cheese thoroughly to avoid graininess. This makes the recipe accessible for those avoiding dairy without losing that luscious tropical flavor.

    ⏱ 10✓ Filling is smooth and creamy without lumps
  9. Step 9

    Clean up is a breeze with this recipe. Just rinse your mixing bowls and utensils right away to prevent cream cheese residue from drying and sticking. Store any leftovers in airtight containers in the fridge. Remember, the crust may soften over time but the flavor stays great for a couple of days. Enjoy every bite fresh for the best experience!

    ✓ Kitchen is tidy and leftovers are well stored

Variations

  • Mango Coconut Lime Cheesecake Cups: Add 1/2 cup pureed fresh mango to the filling for a tropical twist. The sweetness of mango pairs wonderfully with lime and coconut, adding a fruity depth. Expect a slightly softer texture due to the added puree.
    Puree mango until smooth and fold gently into the filling before chilling to maintain creamy texture.
  • Vegan No-Bake Coconut Lime Cheesecake Cups: Replace cream cheese with a vegan cream cheese alternative and ensure coconut cream is full-fat. Sweeten with maple syrup instead of powdered sugar. The flavor stays bright and creamy but with a dairy-free twist.
    Use a high-quality vegan cream cheese and chill the coconut cream well for best texture.
  • Ginger-Spiced Coconut Lime Cheesecake Cups: Add 1/2 teaspoon freshly grated ginger to the filling for a warm, spicy note that complements the lime and coconut. This adds complexity and a subtle zing to the dessert.
    Grate fresh ginger finely and mix thoroughly to avoid fibrous bits in the filling.
  • Chocolate Coconut Lime Cheesecake Cups: Swirl in 1/4 cup melted dark chocolate into the filling before chilling. The bittersweet chocolate contrasts beautifully with the tangy lime and creamy coconut, creating a decadent dessert.
    Cool the melted chocolate slightly before folding to prevent it from melting the filling.
  • Crunchy Nutty Coconut Lime Cheesecake Cups: Mix 1/4 cup chopped toasted macadamia nuts or pecans into the crust mixture for added crunch and flavor. Nuts add a buttery richness that pairs nicely with the tropical flavors.
    Toast nuts lightly to enhance flavor and chop uniformly for even texture.

Pro Tips

  • Always soften cream cheese to room temperature before mixing. — Softened cream cheese blends smoothly with other ingredients, preventing lumps and ensuring a creamy filling. Cold cream cheese can create clumps that are hard to break down, resulting in a grainy texture.
  • Press the graham cracker crust firmly into the cups. — Firm pressing compacts the crumbs, allowing the butter to bind them into a solid crust that holds together when eaten. Without enough pressure, the crust will crumble apart, making the dessert messy.
  • Use fresh lime juice for the best flavor. — Fresh lime juice provides bright acidity and a clean citrus aroma that bottled juice lacks. This acidity balances the richness of the cream cheese and coconut, elevating the overall flavor profile.
  • Chill the cheesecake cups for at least 2 hours before serving. — Chilling allows the filling to firm up naturally, improving texture and flavor melding. Serving too soon results in a runny filling that lacks structure.
  • Toast shredded coconut before garnishing. — Toasting enhances the coconut’s nutty flavor and adds a crunchy contrast to the creamy filling. Raw coconut can taste bland and lacks texture.
  • Avoid over-mixing the filling once lime juice is added. — Over-mixing after adding acid can cause the dairy proteins to break down and the filling to become watery or separate. Mix just until combined for best results.
  • Store leftovers in airtight containers in the fridge. — Proper storage preserves texture and flavor. Exposure to air can dry out the crust and dull the fresh lime flavor.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using cold cream cheese straight from the fridge.
    Why: Sometimes we rush and start mixing right away without softening the cream cheese, thinking it will mix faster.
    Fix: Always plan ahead and let the cream cheese sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes before mixing. This prevents lumps and ensures a smooth filling.
  • Not pressing the crust firmly enough.
    Why: It’s easy to just sprinkle crumbs without compacting them, especially when in a hurry.
    Fix: Use the back of a spoon or a flat glass to firmly press the crust into the cups. This helps the crust hold together when eating.
  • Skipping chilling time before serving.
    Why: Impatience or last-minute plans lead to serving too soon.
    Fix: Chill the cups for at least 2 hours to let the filling set. If short on time, use gelatin to speed up firming, but expect a different texture.
  • Using bottled lime juice instead of fresh.
    Why: Convenience or not having fresh limes on hand.
    Fix: Always use fresh lime juice for the best flavor and acidity. If unavailable, consider adding a bit of lime zest to boost flavor.
  • Over-mixing after adding lime juice.
    Why: Thinking more mixing will make it smoother, not realizing acid affects texture.
    Fix: Mix gently and just until combined after adding lime juice to avoid the filling breaking down or becoming watery.

Storage & Tips

  • 🌡️ Room Temperature: These cheesecake cups can safely sit at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered well. Beyond that, the filling may soften too much and the crust can lose crunch, so keep covered and chilled when possible.
  • ❄️ Refrigerator: Store leftovers in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 3 days. The crust may soften slightly but the flavor remains fresh. Best served chilled to maintain texture and tangy flavor.
  • 🧊 Freezer: Freeze cheesecake cups for up to 1 month. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and store in freezer-safe containers to prevent freezer burn. Thaw overnight in the fridge and stir gently before serving to restore creaminess.
  • ♨️ Reheating: No reheating needed; serve chilled. If slightly too firm, let sit at room temperature for 10 minutes before eating. Avoid microwaving as it will melt the filling and ruin texture.
  • 📅 Make Ahead: Prepare crusts and filling up to 2 days ahead and assemble just before serving to keep crust crunchy. Alternatively, assemble fully and chill overnight for convenience.

FAQ

Can I make these cheesecake cups dairy-free?
Yes! Substitute the cream cheese with a vegan cream cheese alternative and use full-fat coconut cream. Sweeten with maple syrup or another dairy-free sweetener. The texture will be slightly different but still creamy and delicious.
What if I don’t have graham crackers for the crust?
You can substitute with digestive biscuits, vanilla wafers, or even crushed pretzels for a salty contrast. Just make sure to crush them finely and adjust sugar if needed to balance flavors.
How long do these cheesecake cups last in the fridge?
They keep well for up to 3 days in an airtight container. The crust may soften over time but the flavor stays great. For best texture, consume within 2 days.
Can I use bottled lime juice instead of fresh?
While bottled lime juice works in a pinch, fresh lime juice offers brighter, fresher flavor and better acidity which balances the richness. If using bottled, consider adding extra lime zest to boost flavor.
Why is my filling runny after chilling?
This can happen if the cream cheese wasn’t softened properly or if you over-mixed after adding lime juice. Also, too much coconut cream or not enough chilling time can cause runny texture. Make sure to chill for at least 2 hours.
Can I make these in a large pan instead of cups?
Absolutely! Use a springform pan and press the crust evenly. Chill the filling in the pan and slice into squares before serving. Adjust chilling time as needed for firmness.
How do I get a firmer filling without baking?
You can dissolve 1 tablespoon of powdered gelatin in warm water and mix it into the filling before chilling. This helps it set firmly but may slightly change the texture to more mousse-like.
Can I add other flavors to this cheesecake?
Yes! Try adding mango puree, grated ginger, or a swirl of melted chocolate for variations. Just fold gently to keep the creamy texture intact.
