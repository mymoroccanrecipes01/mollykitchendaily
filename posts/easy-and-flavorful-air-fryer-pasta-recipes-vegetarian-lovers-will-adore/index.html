Crispy Air Fryer Veggie Pasta with Fresh Herbs for a Quick Dinner

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 29, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This veggie pasta gets a crispy twist thanks to the air fryer, making it a perfect quick dinner. Fresh herbs and golden veggies add amazing flavor and texture. I love serving it when I want something fast but still cozy.

Introduction

I stumbled upon this air fryer veggie pasta recipe a few months ago when I wanted a quick, comforting meal without heating up the whole kitchen. At first, I was skeptical—pasta in an air fryer? But after trying it, I was hooked! The trick I learned is to cook the pasta just shy of al dente on the stove, then toss it with fresh veggies and herbs before air frying. This creates a crispy, golden texture on the outside while keeping the inside tender. Plus, using fresh basil and a touch of Parmesan really elevates the flavor in a way that feels both fresh and indulgent. This dish is perfect for those busy weeknights when you want something satisfying but don’t want to spend hours cooking. The combination of crispy veggies and pasta with fragrant herbs makes it feel like a treat, not just another quick meal. I love how versatile this recipe is too—you can swap in your favorite vegetables or add a sprinkle of chili flakes for a little kick. It’s quickly become a favorite in my rotation, and I think you’ll love it too!

What You'll Need

4 servings

  • 8 ounces dry penne pasta
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 medium zucchini, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, chopped
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

How To Do It

Step 1
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the penne pasta and cook for 7 minutes, which is about 2 minutes less than the package instructions. This ensures the pasta is slightly undercooked, so it won’t become mushy during air frying. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. Drain the pasta and rinse briefly with cold water to stop cooking. This step is crucial because the air fryer will finish the cooking and create a crispy texture, so starting with pasta that’s just shy of done gives the best results.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pasta should be tender but still firm in the center when bitten
Step 2
In a large bowl, combine the cooked pasta, halved cherry tomatoes, diced zucchini, and red bell pepper. Add minced garlic, olive oil, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Toss everything gently to coat the pasta and veggies evenly. The oil helps crisp the pasta and veggies in the air fryer, while the garlic and oregano add a fragrant base flavor. Make sure the veggies are cut into similar sizes so they cook evenly.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Ingredients should be evenly coated with oil and spices, no dry spots
Step 3
Preheat your air fryer to 375°F (190°C) for about 3 minutes. Preheating is important because it ensures the pasta and veggies start crisping immediately when placed inside, which helps develop that golden texture and prevents sogginess. Without preheating, the food might steam instead of crisping up nicely.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Air fryer reaches full temperature, ready for cooking
Step 4
Place the pasta and veggie mixture into the air fryer basket. Spread it out evenly in a single layer to allow hot air to circulate freely. Crowding the basket can cause uneven cooking and soggy spots. Cook for 15 minutes, shaking the basket gently halfway through to turn the pasta and veggies for even browning. Keep an eye on the pasta towards the end to avoid burning.
⏱ Time: 15 minutes
✓ Doneness: Golden-brown edges on pasta and veggies, crispy texture
Step 5
Once the pasta is crispy and golden, transfer it to a serving bowl. Sprinkle the fresh chopped basil over the top and toss gently. The fresh basil adds brightness and a fresh herbaceous note that balances the crispy, savory flavors. Adding it at the end keeps the herbs vibrant and aromatic.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pasta is hot, crispy, and coated lightly with fresh herbs
Step 6
Optionally, sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese over the top while the pasta is still hot. The cheese melts slightly, adding a creamy, salty finish that complements the crispy texture. If you’re vegan or dairy-free, you can skip this or use a plant-based cheese alternative. Be careful not to add cheese too early in the air fryer, or it might burn.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Cheese is slightly melted and evenly distributed
Step 7
Serve immediately while the pasta is still crispy and warm. This dish pairs well with a simple green salad or crusty bread for a complete meal. If you let it sit too long, the pasta will soften as it cools, losing some of that delightful crunch.
⏱ Time: Serve right away
✓ Doneness: Pasta is warm and crispy when served
Step 8
If you want to add a little extra flavor, drizzle a small amount of balsamic glaze or lemon juice over the top just before serving. The acidity cuts through the richness and brightens the dish. This is totally optional but a nice touch if you have it on hand.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Light, glossy finish on pasta with a hint of tang
Step 9
Clean your air fryer basket promptly after cooking to prevent leftover oil and food bits from sticking. A quick wipe with a damp cloth or mild soap and water keeps your appliance ready for the next use. Proper maintenance helps your air fryer last longer and prevents off-flavors in future dishes.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Basket is clean and dry

Variations

Spicy Air Fryer Veggie Pasta

Add a pinch of red pepper flakes or diced jalapeño to the mix for a spicy kick. You can also toss the finished pasta with a drizzle of hot sauce or chili oil. Expect a bolder, livelier flavor with the same crispy texture.

Note: Spicy ingredients won’t affect cooking time but watch the heat level to suit your preference.

Vegan Version with Nutritional Yeast

Omit the Parmesan and substitute with nutritional yeast for a cheesy, nutty flavor without dairy. This keeps the recipe fully plant-based while maintaining richness and umami.

Note: Nutritional yeast is added after cooking, similar to fresh herbs, to preserve its flavor.

Add Roasted Chickpeas for Protein

Include crispy roasted chickpeas tossed with the pasta before air frying to boost protein and add extra crunch. This variation makes the dish more filling and hearty.

Note: Roasted chickpeas should be pre-cooked and dry to avoid sogginess.

Use Different Pasta Shapes

Swap penne for fusilli or rigatoni for a different mouthfeel. The ridges and twists hold onto oil and herbs differently, changing texture and flavor distribution slightly.

Note: Adjust cooking times if using thicker pasta; test for doneness before air frying.

Add Lemon Zest for Brightness

Sprinkle freshly grated lemon zest over the finished pasta for a fresh, citrusy note that cuts through the richness and adds complexity to the flavor profile.

Note: Add zest right before serving to preserve its aromatic oils.

Pro Tips

Don’t skip preheating the air fryer.
Preheating ensures the cooking environment is hot enough to crisp and brown the pasta immediately, which prevents sogginess. Without this step, the air fryer basket and food start cold, causing the pasta to steam rather than crisp, resulting in a less desirable texture.
Cut vegetables into uniform sizes.
Evenly sized veggies cook at the same rate in the air fryer, preventing some pieces from burning while others remain undercooked. This consistency is key for balanced texture and flavor throughout the dish.
Toss the pasta and veggies halfway through cooking.
Shaking or stirring the basket exposes all sides to hot air, promoting even browning and crisping. This also helps avoid hotspots and prevents burning, ensuring every bite has that perfect crunch.
Use fresh herbs added after cooking.
Fresh herbs lose their vibrant flavor and can become bitter if cooked at high heat. Adding them at the end preserves their aroma and brightness, balancing the rich, crispy pasta.
Cook pasta slightly under al dente before air frying.
Since the pasta finishes cooking in the air fryer, starting with undercooked pasta prevents it from becoming mushy. This technique ensures a tender interior with a crispy outer layer.
Use a light coating of olive oil.
Olive oil helps with browning and prevents sticking without making the dish greasy. Too much oil can weigh down the pasta and veggies, reducing the crisp texture.
Serve immediately after cooking.
The crispy texture is best enjoyed hot. As the pasta cools, it absorbs moisture from the veggies and air, becoming softer and losing its crunch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Overcooking pasta before air frying

Why it happens Many assume pasta should be fully cooked before any further preparation, but this leads to mushy results after air frying because the pasta cooks again in the air fryer.

Fix Cook pasta 2 minutes less than package instructions so it’s slightly firm. This allows the air fryer to finish cooking while crisping the exterior, preserving texture.

Skipping preheating the air fryer

Why it happens People often want to save time and place food in a cold air fryer, but this causes steaming instead of crisping.

Fix Always preheat to the recommended temperature before adding food. This jumpstarts the Maillard reaction and creates the desired crispy texture.

Crowding the air fryer basket

Why it happens Trying to cook too large a batch at once leads to uneven cooking because hot air can’t circulate properly.

Fix Cook in batches if necessary and spread ingredients in a single layer to ensure even heat distribution and crisping.

Adding fresh herbs before cooking

Why it happens High heat degrades delicate herbs, causing bitterness and loss of flavor.

Fix Add fresh herbs only after cooking to retain their brightness and aroma.

Using too much oil

Why it happens Over-oiling can make the pasta soggy and greasy, defeating the purpose of crisping in the air fryer.

Fix Use just enough oil to lightly coat the ingredients; typically 1-2 tablespoons is sufficient for this recipe.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This pasta is best enjoyed fresh. It can safely sit at room temperature for up to 2 hours when covered loosely to prevent drying out. Beyond that, texture and safety decline.

Refrigerator

Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The pasta will soften as it cools, so reheat in the air fryer or oven to regain crispness. Cold leftovers can taste a bit soggy but still work for a quick meal.

Freezer

Freeze the pasta in a sealed container or freezer bag for up to 1 month. To prevent freezer burn, remove as much air as possible before sealing. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.

Reheating

Reheat in the air fryer at 350°F for 5-7 minutes to restore crispness. Alternatively, use a preheated oven at 375°F for 10 minutes. Avoid microwaving as it makes the pasta soggy. Add a light spray of oil before reheating if needed.

Make Ahead

You can cook the pasta and prep the veggies 1-2 days ahead, storing them separately in the fridge. Toss with oil and herbs just before air frying for best freshness and texture.

FAQ

Can I use frozen vegetables instead of fresh for this recipe?
You can use frozen veggies, but make sure to thaw and pat them dry thoroughly before mixing with the pasta. Excess moisture from frozen veggies can make the pasta soggy and prevent crisping in the air fryer.
What if I don’t have an air fryer? Can I bake this in the oven?
Yes! Spread the pasta and veggies on a baking sheet lined with parchment and bake at 400°F for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway. It won’t be quite as crispy as the air fryer but still tasty and golden.
How do I prevent the pasta from sticking together during cooking?
Toss the pasta with olive oil and stir well before air frying. Also, make sure to spread it out in a single layer in the basket. Stirring halfway through cooking helps separate any clumps.
Can I add protein like chicken or tofu to this dish?
Absolutely! Precooked chicken or firm tofu cubes can be added with the veggies before air frying. Just make sure they’re cut small enough to crisp evenly and adjust cooking time as needed.
Why do I need to undercook the pasta before air frying?
Because the pasta cooks again in the air fryer while crisping, starting with undercooked pasta prevents it from becoming mushy or overdone. It ensures the final texture is tender inside with a crispy outside.
How can I make this recipe vegan?
Simply omit the Parmesan cheese or replace it with nutritional yeast for a cheesy flavor without dairy. Also, double-check your pasta is egg-free if you want it fully vegan.
Is it okay to use dried herbs instead of fresh?
Dried herbs can be used during the mixing step to infuse flavor, but fresh herbs added after cooking provide the best aroma and brightness. If using dried herbs only, add a bit more to compensate.
Can I prepare this dish entirely ahead of time?
You can boil the pasta and chop veggies a day ahead, storing them separately in the fridge. Toss everything with oil and herbs just before air frying to keep flavors fresh and textures optimal.
