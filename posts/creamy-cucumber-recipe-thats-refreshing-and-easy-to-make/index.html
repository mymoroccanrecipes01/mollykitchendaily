Refreshing Creamy Cucumber Salad with Dill for Hot Summer Days

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 29, 2026
15 min Prep Time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This creamy cucumber salad is a fresh and tangy side that brightens up any summer meal. The yogurt base keeps it light, while dill adds a fragrant twist. Perfect for BBQs or a quick lunch on warm days!

Introduction

I first stumbled upon this creamy cucumber salad during a scorching summer when I desperately wanted something light but satisfying. The usual vinaigrette dressings felt too heavy or overly acidic for the heat, so I experimented with Greek yogurt for a cooling creaminess. What sets this salad apart is the fresh dill — it adds a bright, herby note that elevates the simple cucumbers and yogurt combo. I also learned that slicing the cucumbers thinly ensures every bite is tender and well-coated with the dressing. Over time, this salad has become my go-to for backyard BBQs and quick lunches because it’s so easy to whip up and refreshingly delicious. The finished dish looks vibrant with flecks of green dill and glistening cucumber slices, making it as pretty as it is tasty. Plus, it keeps well in the fridge, so leftovers are a win too. Honestly, it’s one of those recipes that feels effortlessly fancy but requires almost no fuss.

What You'll Need

Approximately 4 servings

  • 2 large cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons fresh dill, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by washing the cucumbers thoroughly. Then, using a sharp knife or a mandoline, slice them as thinly as possible. Thin slices ensure the dressing clings well and the salad has a delicate texture rather than a chunky bite. Avoid thick slices which can feel watery and less flavorful. If you notice excess moisture from the cucumbers, gently pat them dry with paper towels to prevent the salad from becoming soggy.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Cucumber slices are uniformly thin and slightly translucent with no excess water pooling.
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, combine the plain Greek yogurt, finely chopped fresh dill, minced garlic, and lemon juice. Stir until everything is well incorporated. The yogurt provides a creamy base, while lemon juice adds brightness, balancing the richness. Fresh dill is key here; dried dill won’t give the same punch. Season with salt and pepper to taste, but start light—you can always add more later.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: The dressing is smooth, creamy, and evenly mixed with a fresh herb aroma.
Step 3
Add the sliced cucumbers to the dressing bowl and gently toss them together using a spatula or clean hands. Be careful not to bruise the cucumbers; you want them coated but still crisp. Tossing gently ensures the dressing evenly covers every slice and the flavors meld without making the salad watery.
⏱ Time: 3
✓ Doneness: All cucumber slices are lightly coated with the creamy dill dressing.
Step 4
Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving. This resting period allows the flavors to marry and the cucumbers to slightly soften, making the salad even more flavorful. Skipping this step means the salad might taste a bit raw and the flavors won’t be as harmonious.
⏱ Time: 30
✓ Doneness: Salad is chilled and flavors have blended, with cucumbers slightly tender yet crisp.
Step 5
Before serving, give the salad a quick stir and taste for seasoning. Adjust salt, pepper, or lemon juice if needed. Sometimes chilling dulls flavors, so a final seasoning tweak brightens the dish. Serve chilled for the best refreshing experience.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Balanced flavor with a fresh, tangy, creamy profile and no overpowering salt.
Step 6
Optional: Garnish with a few extra sprigs of fresh dill or a sprinkle of freshly cracked black pepper for a pretty presentation. This little touch adds visual appeal and a hint of fresh aroma that invites you to dig in.
⏱ Time: 1
✓ Doneness: Salad looks fresh and inviting with bright green herb accents.
Step 7
If you want to add a bit of crunch, sprinkle some toasted sunflower seeds or chopped walnuts on top just before serving. This adds texture contrast and a subtle nutty flavor that complements the creamy dressing beautifully.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Crunchy nuts add texture without overpowering the salad’s lightness.
Step 8
For a slight variation, you can add thinly sliced red onions or radishes for an extra zing and color. Soak onions in cold water for 10 minutes beforehand to mellow their sharpness if preferred. This step is optional but adds a lovely complexity to the salad.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Onions or radishes are crisp and add a mild bite to the salad.
Step 9
Serve this salad as a side to grilled meats, seafood, or as a light lunch on its own with some crusty bread. It pairs wonderfully with summer dishes and keeps the meal feeling fresh and balanced. Enjoy immediately after chilling for best texture and flavor.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Salad is cool, creamy, and ready to complement your main dishes.

Variations

Add Red Onion and Feta Cheese

For a Mediterranean twist, add thinly sliced red onions and crumbled feta cheese. The onion adds a sharp bite while feta brings saltiness and creaminess, complementing the cool cucumbers and dill.

Note: Soak onions in cold water before adding to mellow their pungency. Feta should be added just before serving to avoid sogginess.

Spicy Kick with Sriracha and Chili Flakes

If you like heat, stir in a teaspoon of sriracha and a pinch of chili flakes into the yogurt dressing. This adds a spicy layer that contrasts beautifully with the cool cucumbers.

Note: Add spice gradually and taste to avoid overpowering the salad’s fresh flavors.

Vegan Version with Coconut Yogurt

Swap Greek yogurt for unsweetened coconut yogurt to make this salad vegan. Coconut yogurt provides creaminess with a subtle tropical note that pairs well with dill and lemon.

Note: Use plain, unsweetened coconut yogurt to keep flavors balanced and avoid sweetness.

Add Fresh Mint for Extra Freshness

Mix in chopped fresh mint along with dill for a brighter, more refreshing salad. Mint adds a cooling herbal note that’s perfect for hot days.

Note: Add mint just before serving to preserve its vibrant flavor and color.

Include Toasted Nuts for Crunch

Sprinkle toasted walnuts or almonds over the salad to introduce a crunchy texture and nutty flavor that contrasts nicely with the creamy dressing.

Note: Toast nuts lightly in a dry pan until fragrant and cool before adding to prevent sogginess.

Pro Tips

Slice cucumbers very thinly using a mandoline for uniformity.
Thin slices increase surface area for the dressing to cling to, ensuring every bite is flavorful. Uniform slices also promote even softening during chilling without becoming mushy. Thick slices tend to be watery and less enjoyable.
Pat cucumber slices dry with paper towels before mixing.
Cucumbers release water that can dilute the dressing and make the salad soggy. Drying them helps preserve crispness and keeps the salad fresh and vibrant, especially after chilling.
Use fresh dill instead of dried for best flavor.
Fresh dill contains essential oils that give the salad its bright, herby aroma. Dried dill lacks this intensity and can taste flat or even medicinal, which detracts from the fresh profile of the dish.
Chill the salad for at least 30 minutes before serving.
Allowing the flavors to meld during chilling enhances taste and softens the garlic bite. It also lets the cucumbers absorb the dressing, creating a harmonious and refreshing salad.
Add lemon juice gradually and taste as you go.
Lemon juice adds brightness but too much can overpower the creamy yogurt. Gradual addition lets you balance acidity perfectly to your preference.
Use full-fat Greek yogurt for creaminess and tang.
Full-fat yogurt provides the best texture and flavor, helping the dressing cling well to cucumbers. Low-fat versions can lead to a thinner dressing and less satisfying mouthfeel.
Serve the salad chilled and fresh.
Serving cold keeps the cucumbers crisp and the flavors vibrant. Leaving it out too long warms the yogurt dressing and can cause the salad to become watery and less appealing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using thick cucumber slices

Why it happens People often slice cucumbers thickly thinking it’s easier or more substantial, but this leads to a watery, less flavorful salad.

Fix Use a mandoline or sharp knife to slice cucumbers paper-thin. This allows the dressing to coat each slice evenly and prevents a soggy texture.

Skipping the chilling step

Why it happens Impatience or lack of planning leads to serving immediately, but flavors haven’t melded and garlic can be too sharp.

Fix Plan ahead to refrigerate the salad for at least 30 minutes. This resting time is crucial for flavor development and softening cucumbers slightly.

Using dried dill

Why it happens Dried dill is more common in pantries and seems like an easy substitute, but it lacks fresh flavor.

Fix Always use fresh dill for this salad. If unavailable, omit rather than substitute dried, or add fresh parsley for a different fresh herb note.

Not drying cucumbers

Why it happens Many assume cucumbers won’t release much water, but they do, especially thin slices.

Fix After slicing, gently press or pat dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture, helping maintain salad texture.

Over-seasoning with salt

Why it happens Salt is essential, but too much can overpower the delicate flavors and draw out too much water.

Fix Season lightly at first, then adjust after chilling. This prevents over-salting and balances the salad perfectly.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This cucumber salad should not be left out at room temperature for more than 2 hours as the yogurt base can spoil. Always cover it with plastic wrap or transfer to an airtight container if serving buffet style to keep it fresh and safe.

Refrigerator

Store the salad in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. The flavors deepen over time, but cucumbers may release more water, so stir gently before serving. It’s best enjoyed chilled but can be served cold or at room temperature shortly after removal.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended for this salad because cucumbers become mushy and yogurt separates upon thawing, ruining the texture and flavor.

Reheating

This salad is served cold and should not be heated. If you want to refresh it after refrigeration, just stir and add a splash of lemon juice or fresh dill to brighten the flavors.

Make Ahead

You can prepare the dressing up to 2 days in advance and store it separately in the fridge. Slice cucumbers fresh on the day of serving to maintain crispness. Combine and toss about 30 minutes before serving to let flavors meld.

FAQ

Can I use regular yogurt instead of Greek yogurt?
Yes, but regular yogurt is thinner and less creamy, which can make the dressing runnier. To mimic Greek yogurt, you can strain regular yogurt through a cheesecloth for a few hours to remove excess whey. This keeps the salad creamy and prevents it from becoming watery.
How long does this salad keep in the fridge?
Stored properly in an airtight container, this salad keeps for about 3 days. Over time, cucumbers may release extra moisture and soften. Stir before serving and adjust seasoning if needed. It’s best enjoyed within the first day or two for maximum freshness.
Is it okay to use dried dill instead of fresh?
Dried dill doesn’t provide the same fresh, vibrant flavor and can taste medicinal in this salad. If fresh dill isn’t available, it’s better to omit it or substitute with fresh parsley or mint for a different but still fresh herbal note.
Can I add other vegetables to this salad?
Absolutely! Thinly sliced radishes, red onions, or cherry tomatoes can be great additions. Just be mindful that some vegetables may release more moisture, so adjust drying and chilling times accordingly to keep the salad crisp.
What can I do if the salad turns watery?
This usually happens when cucumbers release too much water. To fix it, drain excess liquid before serving and toss the salad again. Patting cucumbers dry before mixing and chilling the salad covered helps prevent this issue.
Can I make this salad ahead of time?
Yes! Prepare the dressing a day or two in advance and keep it refrigerated. Slice cucumbers fresh on the day you plan to serve, then combine and chill for at least 30 minutes before eating. This keeps cucumbers crisp and flavors fresh.
What’s the best way to slice cucumbers for this salad?
Using a mandoline slicer is ideal for paper-thin, even slices that absorb dressing well. If you don’t have one, use a very sharp knife and slice carefully to achieve thin, uniform pieces without bruising.
Can I add garlic powder instead of fresh garlic?
Fresh garlic is preferable for its bright, pungent flavor, but if you must, use garlic powder sparingly. It won’t provide the same sharpness or texture, so start with a small amount and adjust to taste.
