Simple Baked Zucchini with Garlic and Parmesan That Everyone Will Love

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on July 14, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These baked zucchini slices are my go-to for an easy, healthy side. The garlic and parmesan add a cozy, flavorful punch. Great for weeknight dinners or whenever you want a light but tasty veggie fix!

Introduction

I first tried baking zucchini as a way to use up an overabundance from my garden, and honestly, it was a game changer. Unlike frying or sautéing, baking gives the zucchini a lovely tender texture with a slightly crispy edge, which I absolutely love. What makes this recipe stand out for me is the combination of fresh garlic and parmesan cheese sprinkled on top before baking. That simple addition adds so much flavor without overpowering the delicate zucchini. I’ve made this dish countless times for weeknight dinners, and it always disappears fast. One trick I learned along the way is to slice the zucchini evenly and not too thick so they bake uniformly and don’t turn mushy. Finished, these zucchini slices are golden on the edges, tender inside, and have a subtle savory kick from the cheese and garlic. It’s a healthy, fuss-free side that pairs well with almost any main dish, and I think you’ll find it just as comforting and easy as I do.

What You'll Need

Approximately 4 servings

  • 4 medium zucchinis, sliced into 1/4 inch rounds
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs (optional)

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it. This higher temperature helps the zucchini slices develop a golden, slightly crispy edge instead of steaming and getting soggy. Make sure the oven is fully preheated before baking to get the best texture. Avoid crowding the zucchini slices on the pan — they need space for the heat to circulate for even cooking.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Oven is fully preheated and ready for baking
Step 2
In a large bowl, toss the sliced zucchini with olive oil, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and dried Italian herbs if using. The olive oil helps the parmesan stick and encourages browning. Be sure to coat each slice evenly for consistent flavor. If you skip tossing properly, some slices might be bland or dry. The garlic infuses the oil, giving the zucchini a subtle, aromatic boost as it bakes.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: All zucchini slices are evenly coated with oil and seasoning
Step 3
Arrange the zucchini slices in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Overlapping will cause steaming and sogginess, so keep them spaced just enough so air can circulate. This step is crucial for getting those edges crispy and golden. If you want, you can use two baking sheets to avoid overcrowding.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini slices laid out evenly without overlapping
Step 4
Sprinkle the grated parmesan cheese evenly over the zucchini slices. Parmesan melts and crisps up beautifully in the oven, adding a salty, nutty flavor that pairs perfectly with the garlic. Don’t skip this step—it really elevates the dish from plain baked veggies to something special. Use freshly grated parmesan for best results; pre-grated can be less flavorful and won’t melt as nicely.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Parmesan is evenly distributed over all slices
Step 5
Place the baking sheet in the oven and bake for 20-25 minutes. Around the 15-minute mark, check the zucchini and rotate the pan for even cooking. The zucchini should be tender but not mushy, and the parmesan should be golden and crispy on the edges. If you bake too long, the zucchini can become overly soft and watery, so keep an eye on it.
⏱ Time: 20-25 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini is tender, edges golden brown, parmesan crisped
Step 6
Once baked, remove from oven and let the zucchini cool for a few minutes before serving. This resting time allows the cheese to set slightly, making the slices easier to handle and enhancing the texture. Serving immediately while warm is best, as the flavors are most vibrant and the texture is just right.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini slightly cooled but still warm and flavorful
Step 7
Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Sometimes a tiny sprinkle of extra salt or a dash of freshly ground pepper brightens the dish. If you like a bit of heat, a pinch of crushed red pepper flakes works wonders here.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Balanced seasoning with a flavor you enjoy
Step 8
Serve your baked zucchini as a side to grilled meats, pasta, or on its own with a squeeze of lemon juice for freshness. It’s also fantastic tossed into salads or grain bowls for extra texture and flavor. Leftovers reheat well in a toaster oven or under the broiler to restore crispiness.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Dish plated and ready to enjoy
Step 9
For an extra touch, garnish with chopped fresh parsley or basil right before serving. It adds a pop of color and a fresh herbal note that complements the garlic and parmesan beautifully. Plus, it makes the dish look even more inviting!
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Fresh herbs sprinkled on top, visually appealing

Variations

Spicy Baked Zucchini with Red Pepper Flakes

Add a pinch of crushed red pepper flakes to the garlic and olive oil mixture before tossing the zucchini. This gives the dish a gentle kick of heat without overpowering the delicate zucchini flavor. It’s perfect if you like a little spice with your veggies.

Note: Be cautious with the amount of red pepper flakes to avoid making the dish too spicy; start with 1/4 teaspoon and adjust to taste.

Herbed Zucchini with Fresh Basil and Oregano

Swap dried Italian herbs for freshly chopped basil and oregano sprinkled on top after baking. This fresh herb addition brightens the dish and adds a garden-fresh aroma that pairs beautifully with the parmesan and garlic.

Note: Add fresh herbs after baking to preserve their bright flavor and color.

Zucchini Parmesan Gratin

Layer the zucchini slices in a baking dish, sprinkle with parmesan and mozzarella, and bake until bubbly and golden brown on top. This variation turns the simple side into a richer, cheesy gratin perfect for comfort food cravings.

Note: Bake covered for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 10 minutes to brown the cheese.

Vegan Baked Zucchini with Nutritional Yeast

Replace parmesan with nutritional yeast and use garlic-infused olive oil to keep the flavor rich while making the recipe vegan-friendly. Nutritional yeast adds a cheesy, nutty flavor without dairy.

Note: Nutritional yeast doesn’t melt like cheese, so expect a different texture but similar umami flavor.

Lemon Garlic Baked Zucchini

Add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and zest to the garlic and olive oil mixture before baking. The lemon adds a bright, fresh tang that balances the richness of the parmesan and elevates the flavors.

Note: Add lemon zest before baking but squeeze fresh lemon juice over the zucchini right after baking for maximum brightness.

Pro Tips

Slice zucchini evenly about 1/4 inch thick for consistent cooking.
Uniform slices ensure all pieces cook at the same rate, preventing some from being mushy while others remain undercooked. Uneven slices can lead to unpredictable texture and flavor, so take the time to slice carefully.
Use freshly grated parmesan for best melting and crisping results.
Freshly grated parmesan melts smoothly and crisps beautifully, enhancing both texture and flavor. Pre-grated parmesan often contains additives that prevent melting, resulting in a less appealing finish.
Don’t overcrowd the baking sheet; give zucchini space to crisp.
Crowding traps steam and causes the zucchini to steam rather than bake, leading to soggy results. Proper spacing allows hot air to circulate, creating those desirable crispy edges.
Preheat the oven fully before baking the zucchini.
A fully heated oven ensures immediate cooking and browning, which helps develop texture and flavor. Starting in a cooler oven can make the zucchini release too much water and become mushy.
Rotate the baking sheet halfway through baking for even cooking.
Ovens often have hot spots; turning the pan ensures all slices brown evenly and prevents some from burning or undercooking.
Let zucchini cool slightly before serving to set the cheese.
A short resting period allows the parmesan to firm up, making it easier to handle and enhancing the texture contrast between crispy cheese and tender zucchini.
Add a sprinkle of fresh herbs like parsley for brightness before serving.
Fresh herbs add color and a fresh flavor note that balances the richness of the parmesan and garlic, making the dish more visually appealing and flavorful.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Slicing zucchini too thick or unevenly

Why it happens People often slice zucchini quickly without measuring, leading to uneven cooking times and texture differences.

Fix Use a sharp knife or mandoline to slice evenly around 1/4 inch thick. This ensures all pieces cook uniformly and develop the ideal tender-crisp texture.

Overcrowding the baking sheet

Why it happens Trying to bake all zucchini at once on a small pan leads to slices overlapping and steaming instead of roasting.

Fix Use two baking sheets if needed, or bake in batches. Spacing slices allows hot air to circulate and creates crispy edges.

Using pre-grated parmesan cheese

Why it happens Pre-grated parmesan often contains anti-caking agents that prevent proper melting and browning.

Fix Grate fresh parmesan yourself for better flavor and texture. It crisps and melts much better during baking.

Not preheating the oven fully

Why it happens Starting to bake before the oven reaches the correct temperature can cause soggy zucchini due to slow cooking.

Fix Always wait until the oven is fully preheated to 425°F before placing zucchini inside for optimal texture.

Skipping the oil or garlic

Why it happens Some try to reduce fat or simplify but removing oil or garlic results in bland, dry zucchini.

Fix Use olive oil to promote browning and flavor, and minced garlic to add aromatic depth. They’re key to making this dish tasty.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Baked zucchini can be safely kept at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered loosely with foil or a clean kitchen towel. Beyond that, store it in the fridge to prevent spoilage.

Refrigerator

Store leftover baked zucchini in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. The texture may soften slightly when cold, so I recommend reheating in a toaster oven or oven to restore crispiness before serving.

Freezer

Freeze baked zucchini in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to a freezer-safe bag or container for up to 2 months. Freeze uncovered initially to prevent sogginess, then wrap tightly. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat in the oven for best texture.

Reheating

Reheat baked zucchini in a preheated oven at 375°F for 10-15 minutes until warmed through and edges crisp up again. Avoid microwaving as it can make the zucchini mushy. Adding a light drizzle of olive oil before reheating helps restore moisture and flavor.

Make Ahead

You can slice and season the zucchini up to 1 day in advance, storing it covered in the fridge. Bake just before serving for the freshest texture and flavor. This prep step saves time on busy days.

FAQ

Can I use frozen zucchini for this recipe?
Frozen zucchini tends to be watery and mushy when thawed, which affects texture and browning. If you must use frozen, thaw completely and pat very dry before baking, but fresh zucchini is definitely best for this recipe.
What can I substitute for parmesan cheese?
Grated Pecorino Romano or Asiago cheese work well as substitutes, offering similar salty, nutty flavors. For a dairy-free option, nutritional yeast adds a cheesy flavor but won’t crisp the same way.
How do I prevent the zucchini from becoming soggy?
Avoid overcrowding on the baking sheet and bake at a high temperature so moisture evaporates quickly. Also, slice evenly and not too thick to ensure proper cooking and crisp edges.
Can I add other vegetables to this dish?
Yes! Thinly sliced eggplant, bell peppers, or cherry tomatoes can be added, but adjust baking time accordingly. Keep in mind some veggies release more water and may soften the zucchini.
Is this recipe suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! You can prepare the zucchini slices ahead and bake right before eating. Leftovers reheat well and make an easy, healthy side for multiple meals.
What’s the best way to reheat leftovers?
Reheat in a toaster oven or conventional oven at 375°F for 10-15 minutes to restore crispiness. Avoid microwaving as it can make the texture mushy.
Can I make this recipe vegan?
Yes, replace parmesan with nutritional yeast and use garlic-infused olive oil. The flavor will be slightly different but still delicious and savory.
How do I get the parmesan cheese crispy and golden?
Use freshly grated parmesan and bake at a high temperature without covering the zucchini. This allows the cheese to melt and brown properly, creating a crispy crust.
