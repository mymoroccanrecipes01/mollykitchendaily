Cozy Fall Food Plot Mix with Roasted Nuts and Cranberries

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on August 12, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This cozy fall snack mix blends roasted nuts, pumpkin seeds, and sweet cranberries for the perfect crunch-chew combo. It’s my go-to for afternoon pick-me-ups or casual get-togethers.

Introduction

I first stumbled upon this fall food plot mix a few years ago when I wanted a snack that felt seasonal but wasn’t overly complicated. What I love about this recipe is how it balances crunchy roasted nuts with the chewy sweetness of dried cranberries and pumpkin seeds. I usually make a big batch to keep around for those afternoons when I need a little pick-me-up or when friends drop by unexpectedly. What sets this mix apart is the subtle hint of cinnamon and a touch of maple syrup that brings everything together without being overpowering. The roasting step is key—it deepens the flavors and adds a toasty aroma that fills the kitchen and makes it feel like fall no matter the weather outside. This mix is great for casual snacking, hiking, or even as a crunchy salad topper. It’s simple, satisfying, and something I find myself reaching for time and time again.

What You'll Need

Approximately 5 cups, 6 servings

  • 1 cup roasted almonds, unsalted
  • 1 cup roasted pecans
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin seeds, roasted
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/4 cup sunflower seeds
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Spread the almonds, pecans, pumpkin seeds, and sunflower seeds evenly on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Roasting the nuts and seeds enhances their natural oils and flavors, giving the mix a rich, toasty base. Keep an eye on them to avoid burning; they should turn golden and smell fragrant after about 10-12 minutes. This step is crucial because raw nuts can taste bland and lack that satisfying crunch.
⏱ Time: 12 minutes
✓ Doneness: Nuts and seeds are golden brown with a warm, nutty aroma
Step 2
While the nuts are roasting, combine the maple syrup, sea salt, and ground cinnamon in a small bowl. This mixture will add a subtle sweetness and warm spice that complements the nuts and cranberries perfectly. Stir until well blended. Don’t skip this step—it helps bind the flavors and gives the snack mix its cozy fall vibe.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Syrup mixture is smooth and evenly combined
Step 3
Once the nuts and seeds are roasted, remove the baking sheet from the oven and immediately drizzle the maple syrup mixture over them. Use a spatula to gently toss everything together while the nuts are still warm so the syrup coats evenly. This helps the flavors meld and creates a slight sticky texture that holds the mix together without clumping. Be careful not to overmix or crush the nuts.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Nuts and seeds are evenly coated with syrup, slightly glossy
Step 4
Transfer the coated nuts and seeds to a large mixing bowl and let them cool completely. Cooling is essential to allow the syrup to set and the flavors to develop fully. If you add the dried cranberries too early or while the mix is still hot, they can become soggy, which changes the texture of the snack mix.
⏱ Time: 20 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mix is cool to touch and syrup feels set
Step 5
Once the mix has cooled, fold in the dried cranberries gently. These add a chewy, tart contrast that balances the crunchy nuts and seeds. Adding them last preserves their texture and flavor. Avoid stirring too vigorously to keep the cranberries intact and evenly distributed throughout the mix.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cranberries are evenly dispersed without breaking
Step 6
Taste the mix and adjust the seasoning if needed. If you prefer it sweeter, add a little more maple syrup or a sprinkle of brown sugar and toss gently. For a saltier kick, a pinch more sea salt can enhance the flavors. This final tweak lets you personalize the snack to your liking.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Balanced flavor between sweet, salty, and spicy
Step 7
Store the finished mix in an airtight container at room temperature. Keeping it sealed helps maintain the crunch and prevents the dried fruit from drying out further. This snack mix stays fresh for up to two weeks, making it perfect for meal prep or spontaneous snacking.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Mix stored in sealed container, ready to enjoy
Step 8
To serve, simply scoop out a handful whenever you need a quick energy boost. This mix is great on its own or sprinkled over yogurt or oatmeal for added texture and flavor. It’s versatile and easy to customize with your favorite nuts or dried fruits.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Snack mix ready to eat or garnish other dishes
Step 9
If you want to elevate the mix further, try adding a pinch of cayenne pepper or a dash of vanilla extract to the syrup before coating. These subtle additions can add warmth or sweetness that surprises your taste buds and makes the mix even more unique. Experiment and find your favorite combo!
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Flavors enhanced with optional spices or extracts

Variations

Spicy Maple Nut Mix

Add a pinch of cayenne pepper and smoked paprika to the maple syrup before coating the nuts. This gives the mix a smoky, spicy kick that’s perfect if you want a little heat with your sweet. Expect a pleasantly warm aftertaste that balances the sweetness beautifully.

Note: Be careful with the cayenne quantity; start small to avoid overpowering the natural nut flavors.

Chocolate Drizzled Fall Mix

Once the mix is cooled, drizzle melted dark chocolate over it and let it set. This variation adds a rich, indulgent layer without overwhelming the natural nutty and fruity flavors. It’s a crowd-pleaser for holiday gatherings or gift jars.

Note: Allow the mix to cool fully before adding chocolate to prevent melting and clumping.

Tropical Twist Snack Mix

Swap cranberries for dried pineapple and mango chunks, and add toasted coconut flakes. This brightens the flavor profile with tropical sweetness and chewy texture, making it feel summery and fresh despite the fall theme.

Note: Use unsweetened dried fruit to avoid excess sugar and balance flavors.

Savory Herb Nut Mix

Replace cinnamon with rosemary and thyme, skip the maple syrup, and add a sprinkle of smoked sea salt after roasting. This savory version works great as a cocktail snack or party appetizer with a fresh herbal aroma.

Note: Coat nuts lightly with olive oil before roasting to help herbs stick and intensify flavor.

Gluten-Free Granola Blend

Add gluten-free rolled oats and a handful of chia seeds to the mix before roasting. This creates a granola-style snack that’s hearty and filling, perfect for breakfast or trail mix.

Note: Roast oats separately or with nuts to avoid burning, stirring frequently.

Pro Tips

Don’t overcrowd the baking sheet when roasting nuts and seeds.
Overcrowding traps steam and prevents even roasting, leading to soggy or unevenly toasted nuts. Spread them in a single layer so hot air circulates well, ensuring consistent browning and maximum crunch.
Cool the nuts completely before adding dried fruit.
Adding dried fruit while the mix is still warm causes the fruit to sweat and become mushy. Cooling preserves the fruit’s chewy texture and prevents moisture buildup, keeping the mix fresh and crunchy.
Use parchment paper to line your baking sheet.
Parchment prevents sticking and makes cleanup easier. It also helps distribute heat evenly, reducing the chance of burnt spots on your nuts and seeds.
Gently toss the mix when coating with syrup to avoid breaking nuts.
Aggressive mixing can crush nuts, creating a powdery texture. Light folding ensures each piece is coated without damaging the crunch.
Store the mix in an airtight container at room temperature.
Exposure to air causes the nuts to go stale and dried fruit to harden. Airtight storage preserves freshness, flavor, and texture for up to two weeks.
Experiment with adding a pinch of cayenne for a spicy kick.
A little heat contrasts nicely with the sweetness and adds complexity. It’s a simple way to elevate the mix and surprise your palate.
Use a mix of nuts for varied texture and flavor.
Different nuts offer unique crunch and taste profiles, making the snack more interesting and satisfying with every bite.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the roasting step

Why it happens It’s tempting to save time, but some assume raw nuts are fine as-is for snack mixes.

Fix Always roast nuts and seeds to unlock their full flavor and crunch. Even a quick 10-12 minute roast makes a huge difference in taste and aroma.

Adding dried fruit too early

Why it happens Some think mixing everything at once saves a step, but they don’t realize it ruins texture.

Fix Wait until the mix cools completely before folding in dried cranberries to keep them chewy and fresh.

Using pre-roasted or salted nuts without adjusting salt

Why it happens It’s easy to grab whatever nuts are on hand without considering seasoning impact.

Fix Use raw, unsalted nuts and control salt levels yourself to avoid an overly salty mix.

Overmixing when coating with syrup

Why it happens People want to coat every piece evenly but get too aggressive.

Fix Toss gently to avoid breaking nuts and keep the mix’s texture intact.

Storing mix uncovered or in humid conditions

Why it happens Sometimes snacks are left out or in loosely closed containers.

Fix Store in airtight containers in a cool, dry place to maintain crunch and prevent spoilage.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Keep the snack mix in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks. Avoid humid or warm spots as moisture can cause the nuts to lose crunch and the dried fruit to become sticky or moldy.

Refrigerator

You can refrigerate the mix to extend freshness up to a month. The nuts may firm up slightly and dried fruit can harden, so bring to room temperature before serving for best texture.

Freezer

Freeze in a sealed freezer-safe bag or container for up to three months. Thaw at room temperature and stir gently to refresh texture. Freezing preserves freshness but can slightly dull flavors.

Reheating

No reheating needed for this snack mix. If you prefer warm nuts, briefly toast them in a dry skillet for 1-2 minutes, but avoid heating dried fruit to prevent softening.

Make Ahead

Prepare the roasted nuts and syrup coating up to two days ahead, store cooled and sealed. Add dried cranberries just before serving to keep them fresh and chewy.

FAQ

Can I use different nuts or seeds in this mix?
Absolutely! Feel free to swap in walnuts, cashews, or even pistachios depending on your preference. Just keep the total quantity similar and roast them the same way to maintain texture and flavor balance.
What if I don’t have maple syrup, can I use honey instead?
Honey can work but it has a stronger flavor and different viscosity. It might make the mix stickier and more intensely sweet. Maple syrup offers a milder, caramel-like flavor that complements fall spices better.
How do I keep the dried cranberries from getting soggy?
Add dried cranberries only after the nuts and seeds have completely cooled. Adding them too early exposes them to moisture and warmth, which softens their texture and can make the mix clump.
Can I make this snack mix vegan?
Yes! This recipe is naturally vegan as long as you use pure maple syrup or another plant-based sweetener. Avoid honey if you want it strictly vegan.
Is this mix suitable for people with nut allergies?
Unfortunately, the base of this recipe is nuts. You could try replacing nuts with extra seeds like pumpkin, sunflower, and hemp seeds, but the texture and flavor will change significantly.
How long does the snack mix keep fresh?
Stored properly in an airtight container, it stays fresh for about two weeks at room temperature. Refrigeration can extend this to a month, and freezing up to three months.
Can I add chocolate chips to the mix?
You can add chocolate chips after the mix has cooled completely. Adding them when warm will cause melting and clumping, so wait until serving or store separately.
What’s the best way to roast nuts evenly?
Spread nuts in a single layer on a baking sheet and toss halfway through roasting. Use an oven thermometer to ensure consistent temperature and watch closely to avoid burning.
