Crispy Air Fryer Zucchini with Melty Cheese Crust

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on August 4, 2026
15 min Prep Time
12 min Time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These air fryer zucchini bites are my favorite quick snack—crispy on the outside with a gooey cheese crust and tender inside. They’re perfect for afternoon cravings or a simple side dish that feels like a treat!

Introduction

I first made these air fryer zucchini bites on a whim when I was craving something crispy but didn’t want to heat up the oven or make a greasy snack. The air fryer was perfect for getting the zucchini tender inside but with a golden, cheesy crust outside. Over time, I tweaked the cheese blend and the breadcrumb coating to get just the right crunch and flavor balance. What makes this version stand out is the combo of Parmesan and mozzarella cheeses—Parmesan adds a sharp, nutty flavor while mozzarella melts beautifully for that gooey texture. Plus, I use panko breadcrumbs for a lighter, crispier crust instead of traditional breadcrumbs. The garlic powder and smoked paprika punch up the flavor just enough without overpowering the zucchini’s freshness. When you bite into these, you’ll get the satisfying crisp outside, then the soft, cheesy zucchini center that feels indulgent but still fresh. These are perfect for an afternoon snack, a simple side with dinner, or even a party appetizer. I love that they’re easy to make and come together in under 30 minutes, which is a win for busy days or last-minute cravings. I’ve had friends ask for this recipe over and over since it’s such a crowd-pleaser, so I’m excited to share it with you!

What You'll Need

Approximately 24 zucchini bites, 4 servings

  • 2 medium zucchini, sliced into 1/4-inch thick rounds
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese, freshly grated
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • Cooking spray or olive oil spray

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by slicing your zucchini into even 1/4-inch thick rounds. This thickness is key because it ensures the zucchini cooks evenly in the air fryer without becoming mushy or drying out. If the slices are too thin, they’ll crisp up too much and lose their tender center; too thick, and they might stay undercooked inside. After slicing, pat the zucchini dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture. This step helps the coating stick better and prevents sogginess during cooking.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini slices feel dry to the touch and are all roughly the same thickness
Step 2
In a shallow bowl, whisk the beaten egg until smooth. In another bowl, combine the panko breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. The panko gives a light crunch, while the Parmesan adds flavor and helps the crust brown beautifully. Mix these dry ingredients well to distribute the spices evenly. When you dip the zucchini into the egg, it creates a sticky surface for the breadcrumb mixture to cling to, ensuring a uniform crust.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Breadcrumb mixture is well combined with visible specks of seasoning and cheese
Step 3
Dip each zucchini slice into the beaten egg, making sure it’s fully coated, then press it into the breadcrumb mixture. Don’t just sprinkle the crumbs on; pressing firmly helps the coating stick during air frying. Place each coated slice on a wire rack or plate and repeat until all zucchini slices are coated. If you want extra crispiness, you can double dip—dip again in egg and breadcrumbs for a thicker crust, but keep in mind it will add more cooking time.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Each zucchini slice is evenly coated with a thick, crumbly crust
Step 4
Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for about 3 minutes. Preheating is important because it jump-starts the crisping process, giving you that golden crust right away. If you skip this, the zucchini might release moisture before the crust sets, resulting in soggy bites. Once preheated, lightly spray the air fryer basket with cooking spray to prevent sticking.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Air fryer basket is lightly coated, and temperature is steady at 400°F
Step 5
Arrange the coated zucchini slices in a single layer in the air fryer basket, making sure they don’t overlap. Crowding the basket traps steam and prevents crisping. You might need to cook in batches depending on your air fryer size. Lightly spray the tops of the zucchini with cooking spray or olive oil spray; this helps the crust brown evenly and adds a little extra crunch without extra calories.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Slices are spaced evenly and lightly coated with spray, ready for cooking
Step 6
Air fry the zucchini at 400°F for 10-12 minutes, flipping halfway through. Flipping is crucial because it ensures both sides get that beautiful golden crust. Keep an eye on them during the last few minutes to avoid burning; cooking times can vary by air fryer model. The zucchini should be tender inside but not mushy, with a crispy, cheesy crust outside.
⏱ Time: 12 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini slices are golden brown and crispy on both sides, tender inside
Step 7
If you want an extra cheesy finish, sprinkle a little extra shredded mozzarella over the zucchini slices as soon as they come out of the air fryer. The residual heat will melt the cheese perfectly without needing extra cooking. This step adds a gooey, melty contrast to the crunchy crust and is my personal favorite touch.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mozzarella is melted and slightly bubbly on top
Step 8
Serve immediately while hot and crispy. These zucchini bites are best enjoyed fresh out of the air fryer for maximum crunch. Pair them with your favorite dipping sauce like marinara, ranch, or a spicy aioli for extra flavor. If you wait too long, the crust can soften as it cools, so timing is key for the best texture experience.
⏱ Time: Serve immediately
✓ Doneness: Golden, crispy zucchini bites are warm and ready to eat
Step 9
Clean your air fryer basket promptly after cooking to prevent cheese and crumbs from hardening. A quick wipe with a damp cloth while still warm makes cleanup easier and keeps your air fryer in good shape for next time. Regular cleaning also ensures no lingering flavors affect future recipes.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Air fryer basket is clean and free of stuck-on residue

Variations

Spicy Cajun Air Fryer Zucchini

Add 1 teaspoon Cajun seasoning to the breadcrumb mix for a smoky, spicy kick. Serve with a cool ranch dip to balance the heat. This gives the zucchini a bold twist that’s perfect for spice lovers.

Note: Spices in the coating may brown faster; watch closely to avoid burning.

Herb and Parmesan Zucchini Bites

Mix chopped fresh herbs like parsley, thyme, and basil into the breadcrumb mixture. This adds bright, fresh flavors and an aromatic touch that pairs beautifully with the cheese crust.

Note: Fresh herbs can add moisture; use finely chopped and pat slices dry well.

Vegan Air Fryer Zucchini with Nutritional Yeast

Replace egg with aquafaba or a flaxseed egg, use vegan cheese, and substitute nutritional yeast for Parmesan. This creates a dairy-free version that still crisps well and tastes savory.

Note: Vegan coatings may be less crispy; increase cooking time slightly and spray more oil.

Air Fryer Zucchini Fries

Cut zucchini into sticks instead of rounds for fry-style bites. Coat and cook the same way for a fun finger food that’s great for dipping and sharing.

Note: Longer cooking time needed for thicker pieces; flip carefully to avoid breaking.

Cheesy Stuffed Zucchini Rounds

Scoop a small hole in the center of each zucchini slice and fill with a mix of cream cheese and shredded mozzarella before coating. This makes a gooey, indulgent snack with a surprise cheesy center.

Note: Stuffed slices take longer to cook; watch for bubbling cheese and adjust air fryer time.

Pro Tips

Pat zucchini slices dry before coating.
Removing excess moisture prevents sogginess and helps the coating stick better, resulting in a crispier crust. Moist zucchini can cause the coating to slide off or steam rather than fry, ruining the texture.
Preheat your air fryer before cooking.
Preheating jumpstarts the Maillard reaction on the crust, creating a golden, crispy exterior quickly. Without preheating, the zucchini might release moisture before crisping, leading to a less crunchy bite.
Flip zucchini halfway through cooking.
Flipping ensures even browning on both sides. Air fryers cook with circulating hot air, but one side can brown faster. Turning prevents uneven cooking and maximizes crisp texture.
Use a mix of Parmesan and mozzarella cheese.
Parmesan adds a sharp, nutty flavor and helps with browning, while mozzarella melts smoothly for gooeyness. The combo balances flavor and texture better than using either cheese alone.
Don’t overcrowd the air fryer basket.
Crowding traps steam and prevents proper air circulation, making the coating soggy instead of crispy. Cooking in batches guarantees each slice crisps up perfectly.
Lightly spray the coated zucchini with oil before air frying.
A thin layer of oil helps the crust brown evenly and adds crunch without deep frying. Skipping this step can make the crust dry and less appetizing.
Add extra shredded mozzarella immediately after cooking.
Residual heat melts the cheese perfectly without overcooking the zucchini. This step enhances flavor and adds a luscious texture contrast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping to pat zucchini dry before coating.

Why it happens People often assume rinsing zucchini is enough and skip drying, thinking the moisture won't affect crispiness.

Fix Use paper towels to thoroughly pat dry zucchini slices after washing. This removes surface water that can cause the coating to slide off or steam instead of crisp.

Not preheating the air fryer.

Why it happens Some think preheating wastes time or isn’t necessary for air frying.

Fix Always preheat your air fryer to 400°F for a few minutes before cooking. This ensures the crust crisps immediately and prevents sogginess.

Overcrowding the air fryer basket.

Why it happens Trying to cook all zucchini at once to save time.

Fix Arrange slices in a single layer with space between them. Cook in batches if needed to maintain crispiness.

Using too thick or too thin zucchini slices.

Why it happens Inconsistent slicing due to lack of a guide or rushing.

Fix Aim for uniform 1/4-inch slices so they cook evenly. Use a mandoline slicer for precision if available.

Skipping the egg wash step.

Why it happens Some skip egg to reduce calories or due to allergies.

Fix Use the egg wash to help the coating stick firmly. For egg-free, try a milk-flour mix but expect less crunch.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These zucchini bites are best eaten fresh but can sit at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered loosely with foil to maintain some crispness.

Refrigerator

Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. The crust will soften, so reheat in the air fryer or oven to restore crispiness rather than microwaving.

Freezer

Freeze cooked zucchini bites in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to a freezer-safe bag for up to 1 month. Thaw in the fridge overnight and reheat in the air fryer for best texture.

Reheating

Reheat in the air fryer at 375°F for 3-5 minutes until hot and crispy. Avoid microwaving as it makes the crust soggy. Spritz with a little oil before reheating if needed.

Make Ahead

You can slice and coat zucchini up to 1 day ahead, storing them in a single layer on a baking sheet covered with plastic wrap in the fridge. Cook just before serving for best texture.

FAQ

Can I use frozen zucchini for this recipe?
Frozen zucchini releases a lot of moisture when thawed, which can make the coating soggy and prevent crisping. If you must use frozen, thaw completely and pat very dry before coating, but fresh zucchini is definitely best for crispiness.
What can I substitute for the egg wash if I’m allergic?
You can use a mixture of 2 tablespoons flour with 3 tablespoons water or milk as a binder, or try aquafaba (chickpea water) for a vegan alternative. Keep in mind that the coating might not stick as well or be as crispy without egg.
Why is my coating falling off during cooking?
This usually happens if the zucchini isn't patted dry or if you didn’t press the breadcrumb mixture firmly onto the slices. Also, skipping the egg wash or using too little can cause poor adhesion. Make sure to press the coating well and allow it to set briefly before air frying.
How do I get the zucchini crispy without deep frying?
The air fryer circulates hot air around the food, which crisps the coating without excess oil. Using panko breadcrumbs, drying the zucchini, and spraying lightly with oil before cooking all contribute to a crispy texture without frying.
Can I make these gluten-free?
Yes! Use gluten-free panko breadcrumbs or crushed gluten-free crackers in place of regular panko. The cooking method stays the same, and you’ll still get a delicious crispy crust.
How long can I store leftovers?
Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days in an airtight container. Reheat in the air fryer to bring back the crispiness. Avoid storing at room temperature for more than 2 hours to prevent spoilage.
Can I add other spices or herbs to the coating?
Absolutely! Feel free to experiment with Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, chili powder, or fresh chopped herbs mixed into the breadcrumbs for different flavor profiles.
Is it better to use fresh or pre-grated cheese?
Freshly grated Parmesan and shredded mozzarella melt and brown better than pre-packaged shredded cheese, which often contains anti-caking agents. Fresh cheese results in better flavor and texture.
