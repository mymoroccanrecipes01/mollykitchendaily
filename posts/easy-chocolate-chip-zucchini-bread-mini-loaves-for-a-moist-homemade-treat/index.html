Moist Chocolate Chip Zucchini Bread Mini Loaves You'll Love

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on August 4, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
8 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These mini loaves are like a hug in bread form—moist, tender, and studded with melty chocolate chips. The secret? Fresh shredded zucchini that keeps everything soft. Perfect for breakfast or a cozy snack anytime!

Introduction

I first stumbled upon zucchini bread years ago when my mom handed me a slice packed with tiny green flecks and a sweet aroma. It was comforting but a bit dry, so I set out to perfect it. After countless batches, I discovered that shredding the zucchini finely and squeezing out excess moisture made all the difference in keeping the bread moist without sogginess. Adding chocolate chips was a game changer — they melt into little pockets of sweetness that surprise you with every bite. I love making these in mini loaf pans because they bake faster and are just the right size for breakfast or a quick snack. Plus, they’re easy to share (or not!). The trick I learned is not to overmix once you add the flour, so the texture stays tender and light. When you slice into these loaves, you’ll notice a soft crumb with just the right amount of chocolate and zucchini flavor. Honestly, they’re one of my go-to treats when I want something homemade but fuss-free. I think you’ll love how simple and delicious they turn out!

What You'll Need

Approximately 8 mini loaves

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, sifted
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded zucchini, squeezed dry
  • 3/4 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease or line your mini loaf pans with parchment paper. This ensures your loaves will release easily after baking. Proper preparation here means you won’t lose those beautiful mini loaves when you try to remove them later. Don’t skip this step because it can cause sticking and breakage.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pans are fully greased or parchment is snugly fitted with no wrinkles
Step 2
In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon. Mixing these dry ingredients well helps distribute the leavening agents evenly so your bread rises uniformly. If you rush or don’t whisk enough, you might get uneven pockets or dense spots in your bread.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Dry ingredients are combined smoothly with no clumps
Step 3
In a large bowl, beat the granulated sugar, brown sugar, eggs, oil, and vanilla extract until smooth and creamy. This step incorporates air into the batter, which helps make the bread lighter and tender. Make sure the eggs are at room temperature to avoid curdling and ensure smooth mixing.
⏱ Time: 3-5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is pale, glossy, and well combined
Step 4
Fold the shredded zucchini into the wet mixture gently. The zucchini adds moisture and a subtle freshness, but you want to be careful not to overmix here to keep the batter light. Squeezing out excess water from the zucchini beforehand prevents soggy bread.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini is evenly distributed without watery batter
Step 5
Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, folding gently with a spatula just until combined. Overmixing can develop gluten and make the bread tough, so stop as soon as you see no more flour. This delicate folding ensures a tender crumb.
⏱ Time: 2-3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Batter is smooth with no visible flour streaks
Step 6
Fold in the chocolate chips evenly throughout the batter. Chocolate chips give bursts of sweetness and melt slightly during baking, creating a lovely contrast with the moist zucchini bread. Reserve a few chips to sprinkle on top if you like a pretty finish.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Chocolate chips are evenly distributed without melting
Step 7
Divide the batter evenly among the prepared mini loaf pans, filling each about 3/4 full to allow room for rising. Filling too full can cause overflow, while too little results in smaller loaves. Use a spatula to smooth the tops gently.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pans are evenly filled with smooth batter surfaces
Step 8
Bake in the preheated oven for about 25-30 minutes. Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick in the center; it should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs but no wet batter. Avoid opening the oven too often to prevent temperature drops that can affect rising.
⏱ Time: 25-30 minutes
✓ Doneness: Loaves are golden brown and toothpick comes out clean
Step 9
Allow the mini loaves to cool in the pans for about 10 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely. This resting time helps the bread set and makes removing from pans easier without breaking. Cooling fully also improves texture and flavor.
⏱ Time: 15-20 minutes
✓ Doneness: Loaves are firm to the touch and release easily from pans

Variations

Walnut Chocolate Chip Zucchini Loaves

Add 1/2 cup chopped walnuts to the batter for a delightful crunch and nutty flavor. The walnuts complement the chocolate and zucchini perfectly, creating a more textured loaf. Great for nut lovers or a holiday twist.

Note: Fold walnuts in with chocolate chips gently to avoid overmixing. Expect a slightly denser texture but still moist.

Gluten-Free Chocolate Chip Zucchini Bread

Swap all-purpose flour for a gluten-free baking blend that includes xanthan gum. This makes the loaves safe for gluten-sensitive eaters without sacrificing moisture or flavor. Baking time may vary slightly.

Note: Use a trusted gluten-free flour mix and monitor baking closely; texture may be a bit crumbly but delicious.

Vegan Chocolate Chip Zucchini Mini Loaves

Replace eggs with flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water per egg) and use coconut oil instead of vegetable oil. Choose dairy-free chocolate chips to keep it fully vegan. These loaves stay moist and flavorful.

Note: Flax eggs require a few minutes to thicken before mixing. The texture will be slightly denser but still tender.

Spiced Chocolate Chip Zucchini Bread

Add 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg and 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves along with cinnamon for a warm, spiced flavor. Perfect for cozy fall days or holiday gatherings. The spices enhance the chocolate and zucchini notes beautifully.

Note: Mix spices with dry ingredients for even distribution. Baking time remains the same.

Lemon Zucchini Chocolate Chip Loaves

Add 1 tablespoon lemon zest and replace vanilla with lemon extract for a fresh citrus twist that brightens the chocolate zucchini bread. This variation feels lighter and perfect for spring or summer snacks.

Note: Use fresh zest and be careful with lemon extract quantity to avoid overpowering the chocolate.

Pro Tips

Always squeeze the shredded zucchini to remove excess water before adding it to the batter.
Zucchini holds a lot of moisture, which can make your bread soggy if not drained. Squeezing out water ensures the bread stays moist but not wet, preserving its tender crumb and preventing a gummy texture.
Don’t overmix the batter once you add the flour.
Overmixing develops gluten, which toughens the bread. Gentle folding just until combined keeps the texture soft and light, giving you that perfect tender bite every time.
Use room temperature eggs and oil for better mixing.
Cold ingredients can cause the batter to curdle or mix unevenly, leading to inconsistent texture. Room temperature ingredients blend smoothly, creating a uniform batter that bakes evenly.
Fill the mini loaf pans only about 3/4 full.
This leaves enough room for the bread to rise without overflowing. Overfilled pans can cause messy spills and uneven baking, while underfilled pans yield smaller loaves that bake too quickly.
Let the loaves cool before removing them from pans.
Cooling allows the bread to set and firm up, reducing the risk of breaking. Removing too soon can cause crumbling and loss of shape.
Use parchment paper liners for easy loaf removal.
Parchment prevents sticking without extra grease and makes cleanup easier. It also helps maintain the loaf’s shape during baking and cooling.
Add a few chocolate chips on top before baking for a pretty finish.
This adds visual appeal and extra melty chocolate on the crust, making the loaves look irresistible and inviting.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Not squeezing enough water from zucchini

Why it happens Many assume shredded zucchini can be added straight without draining, thinking the batter will absorb the moisture.

Fix After shredding, place zucchini in a clean kitchen towel or cheesecloth and squeeze firmly to remove excess water. This step is crucial to avoid soggy bread and ensures a tender texture.

Overmixing the batter

Why it happens Eager cooks often mix vigorously to combine ingredients quickly, unaware this toughens the bread.

Fix Use a spatula to fold ingredients gently just until no flour streaks remain. This keeps gluten development minimal, resulting in a softer crumb.

Using old or insufficient leavening

Why it happens Sometimes baking powder or soda is expired or measured incorrectly, causing flat bread.

Fix Check expiration dates and measure precisely. Fresh leavening agents ensure proper rise and light texture.

Filling loaf pans too full

Why it happens Trying to maximize yield, bakers fill pans to the brim, risking overflow during baking.

Fix Fill pans only 3/4 full to allow space for rising. This prevents messes and ensures even baking.

Removing loaves from pans too soon

Why it happens Impatience to try the bread can lead to breaking if loaves are still too warm and fragile.

Fix Let loaves cool 10 minutes in pan, then transfer to a wire rack to cool fully before slicing.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Store the mini loaves in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days. Keep them away from direct sunlight or heat to maintain moisture. They’re perfect for quick snacking without refrigeration.

Refrigerator

Keep loaves in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days. The bread may firm up and lose some softness, so warm slightly before serving to refresh the texture and flavor.

Freezer

Wrap each mini loaf tightly in plastic wrap and place in a freezer-safe bag for up to 3 months. To thaw, leave at room temperature for a few hours or warm gently in the oven. Proper wrapping prevents freezer burn and helps retain moisture.

Reheating

Warm slices gently in a toaster oven or microwave for 15-20 seconds. Adding a small pat of butter or a splash of milk before warming can enhance softness. Avoid overheating, which can dry out the bread.

Make Ahead

You can prepare the batter a day ahead and keep it covered in the fridge. Bake fresh the next day for best texture. Alternatively, bake the loaves and freeze them, then thaw and warm as needed.

FAQ

Can I use frozen zucchini for this recipe?
Frozen zucchini releases more water when thawed, which can make the bread soggy. If you use frozen, make sure to thaw completely and squeeze out as much moisture as possible before adding it to the batter to maintain the right texture.
How do I know when the bread is fully baked?
Insert a toothpick into the center of a loaf. If it comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs, it’s done. Wet batter on the toothpick means it needs more time. Baking times can vary slightly depending on your oven and loaf size.
Can I substitute the vegetable oil with butter?
Yes, melted and cooled butter can work, but it will change the texture slightly, making the bread a bit denser and richer. Be sure to cool the butter before mixing to avoid cooking the eggs or batter prematurely.
Why does my zucchini bread turn out dry sometimes?
Dry bread often results from overbaking or using zucchini with too little moisture. Also, overmixing the batter can develop gluten, making it tough. Follow the recipe timing and techniques closely to keep it moist and tender.
Can I make this recipe into one large loaf instead of mini loaves?
Absolutely! Use a standard 9x5 inch loaf pan and bake for 50-60 minutes, checking doneness with a toothpick. The texture will be similar, but baking time is longer due to the larger size.
Is it necessary to add cinnamon?
Cinnamon adds warmth and depth to the flavor, but if you’re not a fan, you can omit it or substitute with a pinch of nutmeg or allspice. The bread will still taste delicious but with a milder spice note.
How finely should I shred the zucchini?
Finely shredded zucchini blends best into the batter, distributing moisture evenly without large chunks. Use the small holes on a box grater or a food processor for consistent texture.
Can I make this recipe vegan?
Yes! Replace eggs with flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water per egg) and use a plant-based oil and dairy-free chocolate chips. The texture will be slightly different but still moist and tasty.
