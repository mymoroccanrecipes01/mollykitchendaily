Crispy Air Fryer Zucchini Fries with a Herby Crunch

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on July 27, 2026
15 min Prep Time
15 min Time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These zucchini fries are my favorite quick snack—crispy on the outside, tender inside, and coated with fresh herbs. The air fryer makes them perfectly crunchy without any guilt. Ideal for a healthy afternoon treat or side dish!

Introduction

I've been making these zucchini fries for years now, and they quickly became my go-to snack whenever I crave something crispy but want to avoid the guilt of deep frying. The first time I tried making fries from zucchini, I was skeptical—they can get soggy or limp if not done right. But the trick I learned was using panko breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan and herbs for that perfect crunch, and air frying them instead of baking or frying. This method gives you fries that are golden and crispy on the outside, while staying tender inside. Plus, it’s so fast—perfect for busy afternoons or when friends pop by unexpectedly. I love how the garlic powder and Italian herbs add a subtle depth of flavor that makes these fries feel special, not just a side dish. Trust me, once you try this, you’ll want to keep zucchini on hand just for these fries. They’re the kind of snack that everyone asks for seconds on!

What You'll Need

Approximately 4 servings

  • 2 medium zucchinis, cut into fries
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • Cooking spray or olive oil spray

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by washing and drying the zucchinis thoroughly. Then slice them into evenly sized fries about 3 inches long and 1/2 inch thick. Uniform size is important so they cook evenly and get that perfect crisp on all sides. If the fries are too thick, they might stay soggy inside; too thin, and they could burn or dry out quickly. Patting them dry with paper towels after slicing helps remove excess moisture, which can prevent sogginess during air frying.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini fries are cut evenly and feel dry to the touch
Step 2
In a shallow bowl, whisk the eggs until smooth. In another bowl, combine the panko breadcrumbs, grated Parmesan, dried Italian herbs, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. The Parmesan adds a savory, nutty flavor and helps the coating crisp up beautifully. Mixing the herbs and spices into the breadcrumb mixture ensures every fry is flavorful. Don't skip this step—it's what turns simple zucchini into something special.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Breadcrumb mixture is evenly blended with no clumps
Step 3
Dip each zucchini fry into the beaten eggs, allowing any excess to drip off, then coat thoroughly in the breadcrumb mixture. Press gently so the coating sticks well. This double-dip method is key to getting that crispy crust. If you rush this or don't press enough, the coating may fall off during cooking. Set each coated fry on a plate or baking sheet as you go to keep them from sticking together.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Fries are fully coated with the breadcrumb mixture and not patchy
Step 4
Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for about 5 minutes. Preheating ensures the fries start cooking immediately, which helps develop a crisp crust. If you skip preheating, the fries might absorb more moisture and become soggy. Once preheated, lightly spray the air fryer basket with cooking spray or brush with olive oil to prevent sticking.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Air fryer is hot and ready to crisp the fries
Step 5
Arrange the coated zucchini fries in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Avoid overcrowding to ensure hot air circulates properly around each fry for even crisping. You may need to cook in batches depending on your air fryer size. Lightly spray the tops of the fries with cooking spray to help them brown evenly. This step is crucial for that golden finish.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Fries are spaced out without touching each other
Step 6
Cook the fries at 400°F for 12-15 minutes, flipping them halfway through the cooking time. Flipping ensures both sides crisp evenly and prevents burning on one side. Watch the fries closely towards the end to avoid overcooking, which can dry them out. You want a golden-brown exterior with tender zucchini inside. If they look too pale, add a few minutes but check frequently.
⏱ Time: 15 minutes
✓ Doneness: Fries are golden, crisp, and smell fragrant
Step 7
Remove the fries from the air fryer and let them cool for a couple of minutes before serving. This short rest helps the coating set and prevents burns when eating. Serve with your favorite dip like marinara, ranch, or a tangy yogurt sauce. These fries are best enjoyed fresh for maximum crispness.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Fries feel crisp and slightly cooled
Step 8
If you want to keep fries warm while cooking additional batches, place them on a wire rack in a low oven (around 200°F). This prevents sogginess that can happen if you pile them on a plate. Keeping the texture crisp is key to the enjoyment of these fries.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Fries remain crispy, not steamed or soggy
Step 9
Clean your air fryer basket promptly after cooking to avoid buildup of oils and crumbs, which can affect future cooking results and flavors. This simple maintenance step keeps your fries tasting fresh every time and prolongs your air fryer’s lifespan.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Basket is clean and dry, ready for next use

Variations

Spicy Zucchini Fries

Add 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper and smoked paprika to the breadcrumb mix for a smoky, spicy kick. These fries pack more heat but keep the same crunch and tender interior, perfect if you love a bit of spice.

Note: Spices integrate well with the dry breadcrumb mix; no timing changes needed, but watch for browning as paprika can darken quickly.

Gluten-Free Zucchini Fries

Swap panko breadcrumbs for gluten-free breadcrumbs or crushed cornflakes. The texture will be slightly different but still crispy. Great for those with gluten intolerance who still want a crunchy snack.

Note: Use a gluten-free coating that’s similarly coarse to panko for best results; cooking time remains the same.

Cheesy Herb Zucchini Fries

Mix in 1/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar with the Parmesan and herbs for an extra cheesy flavor. These fries have a richer taste and meltier crust, making them irresistible as a snack or side.

Note: Cheese may brown faster; keep a close eye in the last minutes to prevent burning.

Vegan Zucchini Fries

Use a flax egg (1 tablespoon flaxseed meal + 3 tablespoons water) instead of eggs, and nutritional yeast in place of Parmesan. The texture is slightly less crisp but still delicious and suitable for a vegan diet.

Note: Flax egg can be a bit less sticky; press coating firmly and consider an extra light spray of oil before air frying.

Baked Zucchini Fries

Instead of air frying, bake the fries on a parchment-lined sheet at 425°F for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway. This yields a less oily but still crispy version, great if you don’t have an air fryer.

Note: Baking requires slightly longer cook time and turning for even crispness.

Pro Tips

Pat zucchini slices dry before coating.
Removing excess moisture prevents the fries from steaming in the air fryer instead of crisping. This step ensures the coating sticks properly and results in a crunchy, not soggy, texture.
Press the breadcrumb mixture firmly onto the zucchini.
Firm pressing helps the coating adhere better, reducing the chance it will fall off during cooking. This creates an even crust that crisps nicely.
Preheat the air fryer before cooking.
Preheating creates a hot environment that immediately starts crisping the coating. Without preheating, fries can absorb moisture and become limp.
Cook fries in a single layer without overcrowding.
Air circulation is key for crispiness. Overcrowding traps steam and causes uneven cooking, leading to soggy fries.
Flip the fries halfway through cooking.
Flipping ensures both sides brown evenly and prevents burning on one side, resulting in a uniform golden crust.
Lightly spray fries with oil before air frying.
A fine mist of oil helps the coating brown and crisp without adding excess fat, mimicking deep frying results in a healthier way.
Let fries rest a few minutes after cooking.
Resting allows the crust to set and moisture to redistribute, keeping fries crisp longer and safer to eat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping drying the zucchini before coating.

Why it happens Many assume washing is enough and overlook patting dry, underestimating zucchini's high water content.

Fix After slicing, use paper towels to blot each fry thoroughly. This removes surface moisture that would otherwise cause sogginess during cooking.

Not pressing the coating firmly onto the fries.

Why it happens Some rush the coating step or handle fries too gently, leading to crumb loss during air frying.

Fix After dipping in egg and breadcrumbs, press each fry gently but firmly with your fingers to ensure the coating sticks well.

Overcrowding the air fryer basket.

Why it happens Trying to cook all fries at once saves time but blocks hot air circulation.

Fix Cook in batches if needed, arranging fries in a single layer with space between them for even crisping.

Cooking at too low a temperature.

Why it happens Lower temps cook the zucchini but don't brown the coating properly.

Fix Set air fryer to 400°F to trigger the Maillard reaction for golden, crispy fries.

Skipping flipping the fries halfway.

Why it happens Forgetting or rushing cooking leads to uneven browning and texture.

Fix Use a timer to flip fries at the halfway point for even cooking on both sides.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These zucchini fries can be left out at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered loosely with foil or a clean kitchen towel. Beyond that, moisture accumulation can soften the crust and increase food safety risks.

Refrigerator

Store leftover fries in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. They may lose some crispness but can be reheated to regain texture. Serve warm for best taste.

Freezer

Freeze cooked fries in a single layer on a baking sheet first, then transfer to a freezer-safe bag or container for up to 1 month. Freeze quickly to avoid sogginess. Thaw in the fridge before reheating for best results.

Reheating

Reheat fries in the air fryer or oven at 375°F for 5-7 minutes until warmed through and crisp again. Avoid microwaving as it makes fries soggy. A light spray of oil before reheating helps restore crunch.

Make Ahead

You can prep and coat the zucchini fries a day ahead, keeping them on a parchment-lined tray covered with plastic wrap in the fridge. Cook fresh on the day you want to serve to preserve maximum crispiness.

FAQ

Can I use frozen zucchini for these fries?
Frozen zucchini tends to release more water when thawed, which can lead to soggy fries. If you want to use frozen, make sure to thaw completely and pat very dry before coating. Fresh zucchini works best for crispiness.
What if I don’t have an air fryer? Can I bake these?
Absolutely! Baking at 425°F for about 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, is a great alternative. The fries won’t be quite as crispy as air-fried but will still be tasty and healthier than deep frying.
How do I make the fries extra crispy?
Drying the zucchini well, using panko breadcrumbs, pressing the coating firmly, preheating the air fryer, and cooking in a single layer all contribute to maximum crispiness. A light spray of oil before cooking also helps.
Can I prepare the fries in advance?
Yes, you can slice and coat the zucchini fries a day ahead, storing them covered in the fridge. Cook them fresh right before serving for best texture and flavor.
Is it okay to skip the Parmesan cheese?
Parmesan adds flavor and helps crisp the coating, but you can omit it if needed. Consider adding a bit more seasoning or nutritional yeast for a cheesy flavor alternative.
What dipping sauces go well with zucchini fries?
Marinara, ranch, garlic aioli, or even a spicy sriracha mayo complement these fries wonderfully. Choose based on your taste preference for an extra flavor boost.
How do I avoid the coating falling off during cooking?
Press the breadcrumb mixture firmly onto the zucchini after dipping in egg, and avoid overcrowding the basket. Also, flipping gently halfway through helps maintain the coating’s integrity.
Can I make these fries gluten-free?
Yes! Substitute regular panko with gluten-free breadcrumbs or crushed gluten-free cereal. The texture might vary slightly but still yields a tasty, crunchy fry.
