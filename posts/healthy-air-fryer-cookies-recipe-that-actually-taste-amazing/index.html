Crispy Edged, Chewy Inside Almond Flour Air Fryer Cookies

Molly Kitchen Daily
Published on June 18, 2026
15 min Prep Time
10 min Time
12 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These air fryer cookies are my go-to when I want a quick, wholesome treat. Using almond flour gives them a lovely nutty flavor and chewy texture inside with crispy edges. Perfect anytime you crave something sweet but a bit healthier!

Introduction

I discovered this almond flour air fryer cookie recipe a few months ago when I wanted a quick, healthier alternative to traditional cookies. I’d often tried almond flour in baking but struggled with texture—cookies would turn out crumbly or dense. The trick I learned here is using melted butter and just the right amount of egg to get that perfect chewy inside and crispy edges. Plus, cooking them in the air fryer is a total game changer: it crisps the edges fast without drying out the cookie center. I love them for an afternoon pick-me-up or when friends pop in unexpectedly. They look golden, smell amazing, and have this subtle nutty flavor from the almond flour that pairs beautifully with dark chocolate chips. Honestly, these cookies have become a staple in my kitchen because they satisfy my sweet tooth without the guilt. If you’ve been hesitant about using almond flour for cookies, this recipe might just change your mind!

What You'll Need

Approximately 12 cookies

  • 1 1/2 cups almond flour, finely ground
  • 1/4 cup coconut sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup dark chocolate chips

How To Do It

Step 1
In a large bowl, combine the almond flour, coconut sugar, baking soda, and salt. Whisk them together thoroughly to ensure the leavening agent and salt are evenly distributed. This step is crucial because uneven mixing can cause the cookies to bake unevenly, resulting in some being too dense or others too crumbly. Taking the time to mix well helps the dough develop a consistent texture and flavor.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Dry ingredients look fully combined with no clumps or streaks
Step 2
In a separate bowl, whisk the egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract until smooth. The melted butter adds moisture and richness, while the egg acts as a binder to hold the dough together. Whisking the wet ingredients well helps them incorporate evenly into the dry mix, which is key for a uniform cookie texture.
⏱ Time: 3
✓ Doneness: Wet ingredients are fully combined with no separation
Step 3
Pour the wet ingredients into the dry mix and fold gently with a spatula until just combined. Avoid overmixing because almond flour dough can become dense if overworked. The goal is to have a sticky dough that holds together but isn’t too wet or runny.
⏱ Time: 4
✓ Doneness: Dough is sticky and holds its shape when scooped
Step 4
Fold in the dark chocolate chips evenly throughout the dough. This adds pockets of melty chocolate in every bite, balancing the nutty almond flavor. Distributing the chips well ensures each cookie has a nice chocolate hit without clumping in one spot.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Chocolate chips are evenly scattered throughout the dough
Step 5
Preheat your air fryer to 320°F (160°C) for about 3 minutes. Preheating ensures the cookies start baking immediately and cook evenly. Skipping this step can lead to uneven baking and less crisp edges.
⏱ Time: 3
✓ Doneness: Air fryer reaches the set temperature before baking
Step 6
Scoop tablespoon-sized balls of dough onto a parchment-lined air fryer basket, spacing them about 2 inches apart. This spacing prevents the cookies from spreading into each other as they bake. Using parchment helps prevent sticking and makes cleanup easier.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Dough balls are evenly spaced and hold shape
Step 7
Air fry the cookies at 320°F for 8-10 minutes. Check around 8 minutes for golden edges and a slightly soft center. The air fryer’s circulating heat crisps the edges quickly while keeping the inside chewy. Overbaking will dry them out, so keep an eye on the color and texture.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Edges are golden brown and centers look set but soft
Step 8
Remove the cookies carefully with a spatula and transfer them to a wire rack to cool. Cooling lets the cookies firm up to the perfect chewy texture. If you eat them too hot, they might feel too soft or fragile.
⏱ Time: 15
✓ Doneness: Cookies are cool enough to handle and slightly firm to touch
Step 9
Store cooled cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. This keeps them fresh and maintains their chewy texture. If you want to keep them longer, freezing is a great option.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Cookies are fully cooled and stored properly

Variations

Add chopped toasted pecans

Mix in 1/3 cup toasted pecans with the chocolate chips for a crunchy texture and nutty flavor boost. Toasting the nuts enhances their aroma and adds a delightful contrast to the chewy cookie.

Note: Fold nuts in gently at the end; toasted nuts may brown faster, so watch baking time carefully.

Swap coconut sugar for maple syrup

Replace coconut sugar with 3 tablespoons of maple syrup and reduce the butter by 1 tablespoon for a moister, slightly stickier cookie with a subtle maple undertone. This also adds a natural sweetness twist.

Note: Reduce baking time by 1-2 minutes to prevent overbrowning due to extra liquid.

Add a pinch of cinnamon and nutmeg

Enhance the warm flavors by adding 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon and 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg to the dry ingredients. This gives the cookies a cozy, spiced aroma perfect for fall or winter treats.

Note: Spices can intensify quickly; adjust amounts to taste to avoid overpowering the almond flavor.

Make vegan with flax egg and coconut oil

Replace the egg with a flax egg (1 tablespoon flaxseed meal + 3 tablespoons water) and use melted coconut oil instead of butter. The cookies will be slightly softer but still delicious and chewy, suitable for a vegan diet.

Note: Flax eggs provide binding but less leavening, so cookies may be denser and need careful timing.

Add dried cherries or cranberries

Fold 1/3 cup dried cherries or cranberries into the dough for a tart, chewy contrast that pairs wonderfully with chocolate chips. This adds a fruity brightness and extra texture.

Note: Ensure dried fruit is evenly distributed and not too large to prevent uneven baking.

Pro Tips

Use room temperature egg for better mixing.
Room temperature eggs blend more smoothly with melted butter and dry ingredients, ensuring even distribution. Cold eggs can cause the butter to seize or clump, leading to uneven dough texture and inconsistent cookie structure.
Don’t overcrowd the air fryer basket.
Proper spacing allows hot air to circulate around each cookie, baking them evenly and producing crisp edges. Crowding traps steam, making cookies soggy and unevenly cooked.
Let cookies cool fully before storing.
Cooling solidifies fats and sets the cookie structure. Storing warm cookies traps moisture inside the container, causing sogginess and loss of that perfect chewy-crisp contrast.
Use parchment paper to prevent sticking.
Parchment paper creates a nonstick surface that protects delicate almond flour cookies from tearing or breaking when removing them. It also simplifies cleanup and prevents excess browning on the bottom.
Preheat your air fryer before baking.
Starting with a hot air fryer ensures immediate Maillard browning reactions, which produce those golden crispy edges. Baking in a cold air fryer can cause spreading and a less crunchy texture.
Measure almond flour accurately by spooning into the cup.
Almond flour can compact easily, so spooning it into the measuring cup and leveling off prevents packing too much flour, which can dry out cookies and make them dense.
Use coconut sugar instead of white sugar.
Coconut sugar adds moisture and a caramel depth of flavor that white sugar lacks, which enhances chewiness and richness in almond flour baked goods.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Overmixing the dough

Why it happens Many assume mixing longer ensures better incorporation, but with almond flour, overmixing breaks down the delicate structure and makes cookies dense or crumbly.

Fix Mix just until the wet and dry ingredients are combined. Fold gently, especially after adding chocolate chips, to maintain a tender, chewy texture.

Not preheating the air fryer

Why it happens Skipping preheating saves time but leads to uneven cooking and spreading since the dough warms slowly.

Fix Always preheat the air fryer to the set temperature before baking. This jumpstarts the cooking process and creates crisp edges.

Using coarse almond meal instead of almond flour

Why it happens Sometimes almond meal is used mistakenly, which has larger pieces and skins, affecting texture.

Fix Use finely ground, blanched almond flour for smooth, tender cookies. This ensures the best mouthfeel and baking performance.

Placing dough balls too close together

Why it happens In an attempt to fit more cookies, spacing is ignored, causing them to merge while baking.

Fix Space dough balls at least 2 inches apart to allow for spreading and hot air circulation.

Eating cookies too hot

Why it happens Impatience leads to biting into cookies fresh from the fryer, which can be overly soft or fragile.

Fix Allow cookies to cool completely on a rack to set their chewy texture and avoid crumbling.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Store cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. Make sure the container seals well to keep the cookies from drying out or becoming stale. Avoid leaving them uncovered as almond flour cookies can lose moisture quickly.

Refrigerator

Cookies can be refrigerated for up to 7 days in an airtight container. The texture may firm up and become less chewy when cold, so it’s best to let them come to room temperature before eating or warm briefly in the air fryer or microwave.

Freezer

Freeze cookies in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to a freezer-safe bag or container for up to 3 months. Wrap well to prevent freezer burn. Thaw at room temperature for about 30 minutes before serving for best texture.

Reheating

Reheat cookies in the air fryer at 300°F for 2-3 minutes to restore crisp edges and warm centers. Alternatively, microwave for 15-20 seconds but this can soften the crispness. Avoid overheating to prevent drying out.

Make Ahead

You can prepare the dough 1 day ahead, store it covered in the fridge, then scoop and air fry fresh on baking day. This helps flavors develop and saves time. Just allow dough to come to room temperature before scooping for even cooking.

FAQ

Can I use regular flour instead of almond flour?
This recipe is specifically designed for almond flour, which has no gluten and behaves differently than wheat flour. Using regular flour will change the texture and may require adjusting wet ingredients and baking time. If you want a traditional cookie, try a standard recipe instead.
What if I don’t have an air fryer? Can I bake these in the oven?
Absolutely! Preheat your oven to 350°F and bake the cookies on a parchment-lined baking sheet for about 10-12 minutes. Keep an eye on them to get that crispy edge and chewy center. The texture will be slightly different but still delicious.
How do I prevent my cookies from spreading too much?
Make sure your dough is not too wet and chill it briefly if needed. Also, spacing dough balls properly and preheating the air fryer helps the cookies set quickly and prevents excessive spreading.
Can I make these cookies gluten-free and dairy-free?
Yes, almond flour makes them naturally gluten-free. For dairy-free, substitute melted butter with coconut oil and use a flax egg instead of a chicken egg. This will alter texture slightly but keeps the cookies tasty.
Why are my cookies crumbly after baking?
Crumbly cookies usually mean the dough lacked enough moisture or binding. Ensure you use the correct amount of egg and butter, and avoid overmixing. Also, use finely ground almond flour rather than coarse almond meal.
How long can I store these cookies?
At room temperature, they keep fresh for up to 3 days. Refrigerated, they last about a week but may lose some chewiness. Freezing extends shelf life to 3 months when stored properly.
Can I add other mix-ins besides chocolate chips?
Definitely! Try nuts, dried fruit, or seeds to customize flavor and texture. Just fold them in gently to avoid overmixing the dough.
What’s the best way to reheat the cookies?
Reheat briefly in the air fryer at 300°F for 2-3 minutes for crispy edges and warm centers. Microwaving works too but can soften the crispness.
